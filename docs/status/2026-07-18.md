## What this is

Marketing/editorial website for Jordan Yates' cruise-focused luxury travel

  (`@theme` tokens in `src/app/globals.css`), npm

## Architecture notes

- **Feature flags:** `src/lib/flags.ts`. Currently just `FIELD_NOTES_ENABLED`
  (now `true`). A single flag gates every surface of a section at once —
  nav, footer, sitemap, cross-links, and the routes (which `notFound()` and
  prebuild nothing when off). This is the pattern for staging future sections.
- **Content layer:** editorial pieces (destinations, field notes) are authored
  in TypeScript, not the database. Journeys remain Supabase-backed. Each new
  destination/note is a quick data-object addition.
- **OG cards:** `src/lib/og.tsx` renders a shared letterpress plate; each
  route's `opengraph-image.tsx` calls `ogCard({ kicker, title, subtitle })`.
  Fonts read from disk; `next.config.ts` `outputFileTracingIncludes` keeps them
  in on-demand bundles.

## Brand guardrails (always honored)

- The Brand Bible's internal working name never appears in any published
  output (copy, titles, metadata, share images, stamps).
- No booking-engine / tropical / influencer imagery (no palm trees, planes,
  ships-as-clip-art, suitcases, cocktails, beach chairs).
- Color only from the defined tokens; gold never metallic.
- Voice: advisor / explorer / curator — never salesman.
