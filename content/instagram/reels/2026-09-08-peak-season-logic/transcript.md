# Peak-season booking logic, run backwards

**Format:** critique · **Runtime:** ~48s · **Words:** 125 (at 2.6 w/s)
**Hook (first 3s):** "The logic of booking peak season is backwards, and I'll show you where."

## Read-aloud script

> [0:00 — frame 01] The logic of booking peak season is backwards, and I'll show you where.
>
> [0:04 — frame 02] People book July because that is when the Mediterranean is supposedly best. But July isn't the best month. It's the month the schools are shut.
>
> [0:13 — frame 03] So you pay a premium for other people's timetable, and receive the crowd it created.
>
> [0:20 — frame 04] Then it doubles down. Peak week, so book early, so lock the fare, so no flexibility is left.
>
> [0:27 — frame 05] Meanwhile September holds the warmest sea of the year, the meltemi eases, and the holidays are over.
>
> [0:34 — frame 06] The critique isn't of summer. It's of the reasoning.
>
> [0:38 — frame 07] Say what you want from the voyage first. The month falls out of it.
>
> [0:43 — frame 08] And if it must be July, we build for July. Mornings ashore, berths rather than anchorages.

## Delivery notes
- Even, reasonable, faintly amused. The argument is doing the work — no edge in the voice.
- Pause a full beat after "the schools are shut". That line is the whole reel.
- "You receive the crowd that premium created" should land dry, not triumphant.
- Warm up noticeably on the last line: this is an offer to help someone who has to sail in July, not a scolding.
- Caption CTA, not spoken: send this to whoever you would share a cabin with.
- Face for 0:00–0:04, b-roll from 0:04 to 0:34, back to face for the verdict.
