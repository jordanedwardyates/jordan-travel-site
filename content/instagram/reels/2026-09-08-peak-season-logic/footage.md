# Footage plan — Peak-season booking logic, run backwards

Every clip carries a source and a rights basis. No passenger UGC. No named ship.

## 01 · 0:00–0:04 · face
- Jordan to camera, seated, natural window light, cream wall. Shot by Jordan.
  **Rights: own footage.** The hook is an argument being made by a person; it needs the face.

## 02 · 0:04–0:13 · the calendar
- A printed wall calendar or a desk diary with a fortnight bracketed in pencil. Slow tilt.
  Shot by Jordan on his own desk. **Rights: own footage.** Letterpress-adjacent, on-brand,
  and it says "school term" without a single stock cliché.

## 03 · 0:13–0:20 · the crowd the premium bought
- A busy stepped alley or waterfront in full midday sun, shot wide enough that no face is
  identifiable. **Rights: own footage** preferred.
- Fallback: Pexels video `crowded greek island street summer` or `busy old town alley`.
  **Rights: Pexels licence — royalty-free, no attribution.** Reject any clip with a
  legible ship name or a clear face.

## 04 · 0:20–0:27 · locked in
- Static, deliberately airless: a booking confirmation face-down on a desk, a pen set aside,
  a closed ledger. Shot by Jordan. **Rights: own footage.** Use `tone: warn` styling here so
  the muted viewer sees the problem beat.

## 05 · 0:27–0:34 · September
- Warm, flat, late-summer sea; an unhurried harbour; long shadows. **Rights: own footage**
  preferred. Fallback: Coverr `calm sea golden hour` loop. **Rights: Coverr licence.**
- Alternative on-brand texture: an antique Aegean chart, filmed as a slow pan.
  Source Library of Congress or Rijksmuseum public-domain scans. **Rights: public domain.**

## 06–08 · 0:34–0:48 · verdict
- Back to face for "the critique is not of summer", held through to the close, then the
  end card generated by `render.mjs`. **Rights: own footage / own assets.**

## Do not use
- Cruise-line press-centre imagery to illustrate "July crowds" — those libraries licence
  editorial coverage *of the line*, not advisor promotion, and using a line's own asset to
  make a crowding argument is a relationship problem as well as a rights one.
  **Confirm with the BDM** before any line asset is considered.
- Any passenger-filmed crowd or queue footage, however widely reposted.
