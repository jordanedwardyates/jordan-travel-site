# Footage plan — free drinks arithmetic

No cruise-line promotional creative appears on screen. The critique is of a
pricing structure in general terms, so nothing needs to be attributed to a
named line — and no named line's marketing is reproduced.

**Rights basis, in order of preference:** (1) Jordan's own footage, (2) an
advisor-portal or BDM-supplied asset with written confirmation, (3) licence-free
stock. No passenger UGC, ever.

| 07 | Back to face for the verdict, same framing as frame 01 so the reel closes where it opened. | Jordan shoots himself. Cleared. |

**Texture pass (optional, behind text):** a scanned antique chart or engraved
compass rose at low opacity. Source from Library of Congress or Rijksmuseum
public-domain collections rather than stock — closer to the letterpress look
and unambiguously free to use.

**Do not use:** any cruise-line drinks-package graphic, any bar or cocktail
footage, any passenger-filmed clip, any stock shot of an identifiable named ship.
