# Footage plan — The booking that fails on position alone

**Reconstruct, don't repost.** This is a pattern, not an incident: there is no
footage of it and there should not appear to be. Jordan to camera carries the
story, static maritime b-roll carries the mood, and the on-screen text carries
the sequence for anyone watching muted.

Face 0:00–0:08, b-roll 0:08–0:41, face for the finding from 0:41.

## Hard rules observed
- **No passenger UGC of any kind** — the viral mishap clips are owned by the passengers who filmed them, no line can license them, and Jordan's advisor permissions do not cover them.
- No named ship, no livery, no identifiable cabin, no identifiable guest.
- No client is named, quoted or characterised; the script says "the pattern" and means it.
- If a line's own asset is wanted for the swell or anchor beat, request via the trade portal or BDM and **confirm advisor-marketing use with the BDM before publishing** — press-centre terms generally license editorial coverage of the line, not our own promotion.
