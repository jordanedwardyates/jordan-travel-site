# Footage — we'll sort it at the pier

A reaction reel, so the face carries it: Jordan for the open, the turn and the verdict, with three
short inserts to break the talking head. Pure talking head past 30s is the weakest configuration
and this runs 46s, so the inserts are not optional.

**Reconstruct, don't repost.** No passenger footage of terminals, queues, arguments at desks or
pier runners. That material is passenger-owned, unlicensable by any line, and outside Jordan's
advisor permissions. Everything below is his own or licence-free.

## Shot list

- **01 · 0:00 · Jordan to camera, 3s** — tight, level, cream wall. The quoted line delivered flat.
  *Source: Jordan. Rights: own footage.*
- **02 · 0:03 · Jordan to camera, 4s** — same setup, no change of framing; let the warn frame carry
  the visual turn. *Source: Jordan. Rights: own footage.*
- **03 · 0:07 · Static empty berth, 6s** — an empty berth or quayside, bollards and slack mooring
  lines, overcast, no vessel name legible. Locked off; nothing moving but water.
  *Source: Jordan, or Pexels — search "empty berth quay mooring bollard"; Coverr — "harbour water
  loop". Rights: Pexels and Coverr licences, royalty-free commercial, no attribution required.*
- **04 · 0:13 · Desk insert, 7s** — a closed passport and a printed itinerary on a wooden desk, a
  hand resting on them, not moving. Data page never visible. *Source: Jordan. Rights: own footage.*
- **05 · 0:20 · Jordan to camera, 6s** — back to face for the verdict sentence. *Source: Jordan.*
- **06 · 0:26 · Kitchen-table insert, 7s** — four items in a row: passport, insurance certificate,
  a handwritten packing list, a printed flight confirmation. Top-down, warm lamp light. Redact
  names, numbers and any airline or insurer branding. *Source: Jordan. Rights: own footage.*
- **07 · 0:33 · Same table, 7s** — a coffee cup set down beside the four items; a hand ticks the
  list. This is the calm the whole reel argues for, so let it breathe. *Source: Jordan.*
- **08 · 0:40 · Jordan to camera, 6s** — the widest of the face setups, for the close. *Source: Jordan.*

## Line assets
Terminal, gangway or embarkation-hall footage may be available via the trade/advisor portal or
Jordan's BDM (Explora's Travel Advisor Centre, Viking's agent tools). **Confirm advisor-marketing
rights with the BDM in writing before use** — press centres generally license assets for editorial
coverage of the line, not for an advisor's own promotion. Treat as unconfirmed until then.

## Texture
Chart-paper and engraving texture for the warn frames: Library of Congress and Rijksmuseum
public-domain maritime collections. Cleared, free, and on-brand in a way stock never is.
