## Caption

"We'll sort it at the pier." No, you won't — and that sentence has cost my clients real money.

At the terminal nothing is negotiable. The manifest is closed, the arrival windows are set, and the person at the desk cannot renew a passport or produce a visa on your behalf. The pier is where decisions get enforced, not where they get made.

Everything that could be sorted was sorted six weeks earlier: passport validity, the insurance certificate, the prohibited-items list, and a flight with margin in it. Four things, one hour, at your kitchen table.

Do that and embarkation in Athens or Barcelona is eleven minutes of queue, then lunch.

Send this to whoever you'd share a cabin with.
