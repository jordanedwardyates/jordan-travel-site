# Three questions to ask an advisor

**Format:** listicle · **Runtime:** ~35s · **Words:** 92 (at 2.6 w/s)
**Hook (first 3s):** Three questions to ask any travel advisor before you trust them with a voyage.

## Read-aloud script

> [0:00 — frame 01] Three questions to ask any travel advisor before you trust them with a voyage.
>
> [0:03 — frame 02] One. How are you paid, and by whom? The answer should be the cruise line, after I sail.
>
> [0:08 — frame 03] If a fee sits on top of the fare, ask what it buys.
>
> [0:14 — frame 04] Two. Which sailing would you talk me out of? Anyone who can't name one is selling.
>
> [0:20 — frame 05] Three. Who answers at two in the morning in my time zone?
>
> [0:26 — frame 06] Not a call centre. A name, with my file already open.
>
> [0:31 — frame 07] Ask me all three. I'll answer in writing.

## Delivery notes
- Three items, no more — resist adding a fourth on the day.
- Number each one aloud; it is what makes the reel saveable.
- "Anyone who can't name one is selling" is the barb. Say it evenly, no relish.
- Final line is the whole offer: calm, brief, then stop.
