# Footage plan — "Three questions to ask an advisor"

Short and face-led: at 35 seconds this works as talking head plus three inserts. No passenger UGC,
no named ship standing in for a voyage we sell.

## Shot list

**01 · 0:00–0:03 · Jordan to camera (face)**
Source: Jordan shoots himself. Desk, daylight from the left, cream paper behind.
Rights: own footage. State the number "three" clearly — it sets the retention promise.

**02–03 · 0:03–0:14 · Question one insert (b-roll, own)**
Source: Jordan shoots himself — a printed fare summary, a hand laying a second sheet beside it,
pencil underlining the deposit terms. Client details removed before filming.
Rights: own footage.

**04–05 · 0:14–0:26 · Question two insert (b-roll, own)**
Source: Jordan shoots himself — a shortlist on paper with two lines struck through in ink. This is
the "talked out of it" visual and it should be plainly handmade.
Rights: own footage.

**06 · 0:26–0:31 · Night desk (b-roll)**
Source: Jordan's own footage first — a lamp, a handset, a notebook at night.
Fallback: licence-free stock, Pexels search "desk lamp night notebook" or Coverr "night desk lamp".
Rights: royalty-free for commercial use, no attribution required
(https://www.pexels.com/license/). No identifiable people, no branding.

**07 · 0:31–0:35 · Back to face (own)**
Same setup as frame 01 so the reel bookends. Hold still on the final frame.

## Line assets
Not needed here, and not to be used casually: press-centre libraries license for editorial coverage
of the line, not advisor brand promotion. Anything line-supplied must come through the trade/advisor
portal or the BDM — mark as "confirm with BDM" until that is in writing.

## Texture
Chart and engraving overlays from Library of Congress maritime charts or Rijksmuseum public-domain
prints, if a background is wanted behind the numbered frames. Verify each item's status first.
