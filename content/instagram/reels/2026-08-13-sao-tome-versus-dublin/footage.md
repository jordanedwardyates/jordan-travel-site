# Footage — São Tomé versus Dublin

**Reconstruction, not repost — and this one matters most.** Both incidents were
filmed and photographed by the people involved, and news coverage of them is
licensed to the outlets that ran it. We hold no rights to any of it. Do not pull
a single frame from news reports, from passenger phone video, or from the
aggregator sites that reposted either story. The reel carries the facts in
Jordan's voice and in on-screen text; that is sufficient and it is clean.

Neither ship is shown. Naming Norwegian Dawn and Disney in the spoken script is
factual commentary on a documented public incident; showing either vessel would
imply footage we do not have the right to use.

## Do not use
- Any news footage or stills from coverage of either incident. Licensed to those
  outlets; not ours.
- Passenger-filmed video of either group, or of any pier-runner event.
- Cruise-line press-centre and asset-library material for Norwegian, Disney or
  anybody else — those grant editorial use *about the line*, not advisor brand
  promotion, and using a line's own assets alongside a critical account of its
  conduct would be wrong on both rights and taste. **Uncleared. Do not request.**
- Stock footage of a named ship.
