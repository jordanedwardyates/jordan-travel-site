# São Tomé versus Dublin — same lateness, opposite bill

**Format:** post-mortem · **Runtime:** ~70s · **Words:** 181 (at 2.6 w/s)
**Hook (first 3s):** "Two groups, late back to the ship. Opposite bills."

Both cases are on the public record. Nothing here is embellished, no outcome is
promised, and no passenger is named or criticised.

## Read-aloud script

> [0:00 — frame 01] Two groups, late back to the ship. Opposite bills.
>
> [0:04 — frame 02] São Tomé, the twenty-seventh of March, 2024. Norwegian Dawn, mid-way through a twenty-one-day Cape Town to Barcelona voyage.
>
> [0:11 — frame 03] Eight guests were ashore on a private tour — six Americans, two Australians, among them a pregnant woman and an elderly man with a heart condition.
>
> [0:21 — frame 04] They returned after the three o'clock all-aboard. Their operator phoned the ship. The local Coast Guard tried to bring them out to her. They were not permitted to board.
>
> [0:32 — frame 05] No belongings, no money, no medication. They paid for hotels, food and onward travel across several countries to rejoin days later.
>
> [0:40 — frame 06] Now Dublin. Thirty-three Disney guests, also late back — but on the ship's own excursion. The ship sailed on time and the line arranged their onward travel.
>
> [0:51 — frame 07] Same mistake, opposite bill. The only variable was who booked the tour. Norwegian's own position was that these guests missed all-aboard on a private tour.
>
> [1:01 — frame 08] Which is correct. So I never ask whether a private guide is better. I ask whether that particular day has any margin in it at all.

## Delivery notes
- Read frames 02–05 as a report, not a horror story. The details are appalling
  enough said plainly; any performance in the voice cheapens them.
- Full stop and a real breath after "not permitted to board."
- Frame 05 is three short sentences. Say them like an inventory.
- Lift very slightly on "Now Dublin" — this is the turn, and it should sound like
  a page being turned.
- The last line is the whole reel. Slow down, land "margin", stop.
- Face 0:00–0:11, b-roll and stills through the body, face again from 1:01.
