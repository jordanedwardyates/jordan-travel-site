# Footage plan — Three questions before the deposit

Face for 0:00–0:04 and again from 0:33; b-roll across the three items. Keep every clip generic — no legible ship name, no line livery, no identifiable faces without a release.

## Frame 01 · 0:00–0:04 · face
- **Shoot:** Jordan to camera, tight, holding three fingers up briefly then dropping the hand. Static tripod, window light.
- **Source / rights:** Jordan's own footage. Cleared.

## Frames 02–03 · 0:04–0:14 · item one, crew ratio
- **Shoot:** Hands setting out cutlery and folding a napkin, close, shallow depth. Crew hands are ideal but require the individual's permission on camera — otherwise use Jordan's own hands.
- **Source / rights:** Jordan's own footage. Cleared. Written permission required if any crew member is identifiable.
- **Alternative:** Pexels, search `hands folding napkin restaurant`, Pexels licence — royalty-free, commercial, no attribution.

## Frames 04–05 · 0:14–0:23 · item two, dining seats
- **Shoot:** A slow pan across an empty dining room, chairs tucked in, then a single laid table for two.
- **Source / rights:** Jordan's own footage, with venue permission if the room is recognisable. Cleared on that basis.
- **Alternative:** Unsplash stills under the Unsplash licence, search `empty dining room set tables`, animated with a slow push-in.

## Frames 06–07 · 0:23–0:33 · item three, berth vs tender
- **Pull:** Two clips. A mooring line dropped over a bollard on a stone quay; then a small tender crossing open water. Pexels: `mooring rope bollard`, `small boat tender open water`.
- **Source / rights:** Pexels licence. Reject any frame with a hull name, funnel logo or livery visible.
- **Preferred:** Jordan's own phone footage shot from a tender or a quayside — better texture, and no clearance question at all.

## Frame 08 · 0:33–0:36 · face into slate
- **Shoot:** Return to the frame-01 setup, then cut to the rendered end frame.
- **Source / rights:** Jordan's own footage plus rendered slate. Cleared.

## Texture layer (optional)
- Chart-paper and engraving texture behind the numbered frames: Library of Congress maritime chart collection or Rijksmuseum public-domain marine engravings, low opacity.
- **Source / rights:** Public domain — verify each item's individual rights statement.

## Not to be used
- No cruise-line press-centre or trade-portal footage here. Those assets are licensed for editorial coverage of the line, not advisor brand promotion; using any would need explicit written sign-off from the relevant BDM, and this reel does not require it.
- No passenger-filmed social clips of tender queues or dining rooms.
