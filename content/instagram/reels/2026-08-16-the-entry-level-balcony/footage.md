# Footage plan — The entry-level balcony

Face for 0–7s, b-roll through the body, back to face for the verdict from 0:28.
No named ship appears at any point: this is a critique of a category, not of a line.

## Frame 01 · 0:00–0:03 · face
- Jordan to camera, seated, deck plan printed and flat on the table in front of him.
- **Source:** shot by Jordan. **Rights:** own footage.

## Frame 02 · 0:03–0:07 · insert
- Slow push across a printed deck plan, finger resting on a lower-deck veranda row.
- **Source:** shot by Jordan on his own printed plan or a generic sheet. **Rights:** own footage.
- Do *not* screen-record a cruise line's booking page — that is their artwork and their trademark.

## Frame 03 · 0:07–0:12 · b-roll
- Looking straight up at an overhanging balcony soffit — any hotel, apartment block or ferry terminal works. The point is the geometry, not the ship.
- **Source:** shot by Jordan, or Pexels search "balcony underside concrete overhang". **Rights:** own footage / Pexels licence (royalty-free, commercial, no attribution).

## Frame 04 · 0:12–0:17 · b-roll
- A shaded outdoor recess in flat, grey, sunless light. Static tripod shot, no movement.
- **Source:** Pexels search "shaded balcony overcast" or Coverr "grey daylight window". **Rights:** Pexels / Coverr licence.

## Frame 05 · 0:17–0:22 · b-roll
- Lifeboat davits and hull plating, framed tight so no livery, funnel mark or ship name is legible.
- **Source:** shot by Jordan on any sailing, or Unsplash search "lifeboat davit hull detail". **Rights:** own footage / Unsplash licence. If a line's own asset is preferred, request via BDM and **confirm advisor-marketing use with the BDM before publishing** — press-centre terms generally cover editorial coverage of the line, not our promotion.

## Frame 06 · 0:22–0:28 · insert
- Letterpress or typewriter texture with the words "ocean view" and "veranda" set as two lines; slow tilt down. Antique-chart paper underneath.
- **Source:** Jordan's own paper and camera, or Library of Congress / Rijksmuseum public-domain maritime chart scans as the background layer. **Rights:** own footage / public domain.

## Frames 07–08 · 0:28–0:40 · face
- Back to camera for the verdict. Cut once mid-line at roughly 0:34 to a slightly tighter frame so the resolution has a visual turn.
- Optional closing insert: an unbroken sea horizon from a high open deck, no railing branding.
- **Source:** shot by Jordan, or Pexels search "ship wake open sea horizon". **Rights:** own footage / Pexels licence.

## Hard rules observed
- No passenger UGC of any kind. Nothing sourced from social platforms.
- No stock footage of a specific named ship standing in for a real voyage.
- No recognisable faces other than Jordan's; no branded livery in shot.
