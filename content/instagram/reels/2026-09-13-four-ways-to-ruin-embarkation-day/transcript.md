## Caption

Four ways to ruin embarkation day, and all four happen before lunch.

Medication belongs on your shoulder, in its packaging — the suitcase you hand over at the kerb may not reach the cabin until evening. Surge-protected power strips are commonly confiscated at screening, and lists vary by line, so read your own. Passports stay in the bag that walks through the terminal with you.

And the queue: arrival windows are staggered because staggering is the only throughput a terminal has. Take yours, and take the quieter half of it.

Save this for the morning you sail.
