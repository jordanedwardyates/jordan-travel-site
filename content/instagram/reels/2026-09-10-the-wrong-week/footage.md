# Footage plan — The trip undone by the wrong week

**Reconstruct, don't repost.** This is a mishap story, so the rule is absolute: Jordan to
camera, static shore-side plates, and on-screen text carrying the narrative. No passenger
UGC of suspended tender operations exists that anyone can license to us.

## 01–02 · 0:00–0:12 · face
- Jordan to camera, seated, chest-up, cream wall. Shot by Jordan. **Rights: own footage.**
- Hold the frame very still. The stillness is the tone.

## 03 · 0:12–0:19 · the fortnight
- A desk diary or printed itinerary with two weeks in August bracketed in pencil; hand
  withdrawing from frame. Shot by Jordan. **Rights: own footage.**

## 04 · 0:19–0:25 · tender ports named
- The itinerary page itself, slow tilt down the port list. Shot by Jordan.
  **Rights: own footage.** If any line branding is visible, mask it or reshoot on plain
  paper — do not publish a line's itinerary artwork without checking with the BDM.

## 05 · 0:25–0:35 · the wind
- Wind-textured open water, whitecaps, mid-afternoon. **Rights: own footage** preferred.
- Fallback: Pexels video `windy sea whitecaps` or `aegean sea rough water`.
  **Rights: Pexels licence — royalty-free, commercial use, no attribution.** No hull, no faces.

## 06 · 0:35–0:43 · the day that didn't happen
- The strongest shot in the reel and it is a still one: an **empty tender pontoon** or a
  gangway rigged with nobody on it, island visible behind. Locked off, no camera movement.
  **Rights: own footage** — this is a shot worth making deliberately on the next sailing.
- Fallback: Unsplash stills `empty jetty greek island`, `harbour steps aegean`, pushed in
  very slowly. **Rights: Unsplash licence.**

## 07 · 0:43–0:50 · the turn, on the face
- Cut back to Jordan for "Nothing failed. No one was at fault." Shot by Jordan.
  **Rights: own footage.** Do not cover this line with b-roll; the absence of blame is the point.

## 08–09 · 0:50–1:03 · September
- Flat warm sea, quiet quayside, long light, a taverna with its own locals in it.
  **Rights: own footage** preferred. Fallback: Coverr `calm sea evening` loop.
  **Rights: Coverr licence — free for commercial use.**
- On-brand alternative for the transition: an antique Aegean chart or a passport stamp page,
  filmed as a slow pan. Library of Congress / Rijksmuseum public-domain scans.
  **Rights: public domain.**

## 10 · 1:03–1:12 · the two questions
- Face, then the letterpress end card from `render.mjs`. **Rights: own footage / own assets.**

## Do not use
- Passenger-filmed clips of cancelled tenders, pier runners or crowded cable cars. Filmed
  and owned by individual passengers; no cruise line can license them and advisor
  permissions do not cover them.
- Any named ship, stock or otherwise, to stand in for the sailing in this story.
- Cruise-line press-centre assets: licensed for editorial coverage of the line, not for
  advisor brand promotion. **Confirm with the BDM** before using anything from a press kit.
