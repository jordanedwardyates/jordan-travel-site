# The trip undone by the wrong week

**Format:** post-mortem · **Runtime:** ~72s · **Words:** 187 (at 2.6 w/s)
**Hook (first 3s):** "The trip that goes wrong is almost never the wrong ship. It is the wrong week."

## Read-aloud script

> [0:00 — frame 01] The trip that goes wrong is almost never the wrong ship. It is the wrong week.
>
> [0:05 — frame 02] I see the same post-mortem two or three times a season, and it reads identically every time.
>
> [0:12 — frame 03] A couple books the Aegean in the second half of August, because that was the fortnight they were free.
>
> [0:19 — frame 04] Three of the seven calls are tender ports. Nobody mentions that, because nobody asks.
>
> [0:25 — frame 05] Then the meltemi does what it does most August afternoons. One tender operation is suspended, and that port is simply gone.
>
> [0:35 — frame 06] The ship sits anchored in sight of the island. And at the two marquee calls there are several ships alongside, so the cable car is a queue in both directions.
>
> [0:43 — frame 07] Nothing failed. No one was at fault. An August itinerary performed exactly as an August itinerary performs.
>
> [0:50 — frame 08] And here is the part that stings. The same route, a fortnight later, is a different holiday.
>
> [0:57 — frame 09] Warmest sea of the year, the wind easing, the crowd gone home.
>
> [1:03 — frame 10] So I ask two questions before anything else. Which weeks can you actually travel — and which of these calls needs a calm afternoon?

## Delivery notes
- This is a pattern, not a person. Say "a couple" and mean any of them; never a name, never a detail that could identify anyone.
- Low and level for the first thirty seconds. Report it; do not perform it.
- The turn is "Nothing failed. No one was at fault." Slow right down there — three short sentences, three small pauses.
- "The part that stings" is the only line with any lift in it.
- Close plainly, on the two questions. No pitch, no urgency.
- Face 0:00–0:12, b-roll through the middle, back to face from 0:43 for the verdict and the questions.
