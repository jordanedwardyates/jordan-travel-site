# Footage — three fixes for a queasy sailing

Face for the open and the close, b-roll through the three items. Nothing here needs a ship interior,
which is fortunate: cabin footage is exactly the material an advisor cannot license.

## Shot list

- **01 · 0:00 · Jordan to camera, 3s** — half-length, cream wall, no props. Straight into it.
  *Source: Jordan. Rights: own footage.*
- **02 · 0:03 · Deck-plan insert, 5s** — a printed deck plan on a desk, top-down, a pencil resting
  near the upper forward cabins. Static, no line branding visible — redact the header, or use a
  hand-drawn section elevation instead. *Source: Jordan. Rights: own footage.*
- **03 · 0:08 · Same insert, 7s** — the pencil moving down and to the centre of the plan, then
  circling once. The move is the whole point; keep the camera locked off. *Source: Jordan.*
- **04 · 0:15 · Horizon, 7s** — a level horizon from a moving vessel, sea in the lower third, a
  slight rise and fall. Shoot it yourself on any ferry crossing; failing that, *Pexels — search
  "sea horizon from moving boat" or "calm ocean horizon slow". Rights: Pexels licence,
  royalty-free commercial, no attribution required.* Coverr has usable short loops too.
- **05 · 0:22 · Tender versus alongside, 7s** — two shots cut together: a small boat working in
  light swell, then mooring ropes and a gangway on a stone quay. *Source: Jordan, or Pexels —
  search "small boat swell" and "gangway stone quay mooring". Rights: Pexels licence.*
  **No identifiable named ship in either shot** — misrepresenting a ship we sell is both a brand
  and a compliance problem.
- **06 · 0:29 · Jordan to camera, 6s** — back to face for the medical caution. It has to come from
  a person, not from text over stock. *Source: Jordan. Rights: own footage.*
- **07 · 0:35 · Jordan to camera, 5s** — same setup, tighter. *Source: Jordan.*

## Do not use
- Passenger footage of rough seas, sliding furniture or "rogue wave" clips. All passenger-owned,
  none licensable, and the register is wrong for this brand regardless.
- Line-supplied stabiliser or engineering footage unless the **BDM confirms advisor-marketing use
  in writing**. Press-centre terms generally cover editorial coverage of the line, not our promotion.

## Texture
Antique chart paper behind frames 02 and 05: Library of Congress or Rijksmuseum public-domain
maritime collections. Free, genuinely cleared, and closer to the brand than any stock texture.
