## Caption

Three things I tell every traveller who is quietly nervous about motion.

Book low and midship — near the waterline, at the centre of the ship, is where a hull moves least, and the cheapest cabin is often high and forward, which is the opposite. When it starts, go out on deck and look at the horizon rather than down at a phone. And choose an itinerary that docks alongside rather than tendering, because small boats in swell are the real culprit on a Mediterranean or Alaskan sailing.

On anything you might take for it, talk to your doctor. I'm an advisor, not a physician.

DM me your ship and I'll tell you which deck I'd book.
