## Delivery notes

- Open flat, almost bored — the assumption is stated as someone else's line, not yours. No emphasis until "argue".
- Land "Tender boats certainly don't" dry, then a full beat of silence before the port-day sentence.
- "Berth length and water depth" is the credibility beat. Slow down; these are the two facts doing the work.
- "Bigger isn't worse" must sound genuinely fair-minded, not grudging. The critique is of the assumption, not the ships or anyone who booked one.
- Final line rises slightly, then stops. Do not add a sell.
