# Footage plan — Bigger ship, better holiday

Face for frames 01 and 07–08; b-roll through the middle. No named ship appears on screen at any point — this reel critiques a design assumption, not a vessel, and putting a recognisable hull under that voice-over would misrepresent it.

## Frame 01 · 0:00–0:04 · face
- **Shoot:** Jordan to camera, seated, chart or letterpress print behind, window light from the left. Static tripod.
- **Source / rights:** Jordan's own footage. Cleared.

## Frame 02 · 0:04–0:10 · b-roll
- **Shoot:** Overhead of a printed deck plan on a desk, hand sliding it across frame. Any generic plan, or one Jordan draws — no line branding legible.
- **Source / rights:** Jordan's own footage. Cleared.

## Frame 03 · 0:10–0:14 · b-roll
- **Pull:** A busy quayside crowd, mid-shot, faces not identifiable. Pexels search: `harbour crowd walking pier`.
- **Source / rights:** Pexels licence — royalty-free, commercial use, no attribution. Reject any clip with a legible ship name or a recognisable face in the foreground.

## Frame 04 · 0:14–0:18 · b-roll
- **Shoot:** A laid table for two, empty, evening light. Jordan's own or any restaurant he has permission to film in.
- **Source / rights:** Jordan's own footage. Cleared. Get written permission from the venue if it is identifiable.
- **Alternative:** Unsplash still, search `empty restaurant table candlelight`, under the Unsplash licence, held with a slow push-in.

## Frame 05 · 0:18–0:26 · b-roll, the turn
- **Pull:** Two shots cut together: a small tender crossing open water toward shore, then a mooring line being made fast on a stone quay. Pexels: `tender boat approaching shore`, `mooring rope bollard quay`.
- **Source / rights:** Pexels licence. Both must be generic — no hull names, no livery.
- **Preferred:** Jordan's own phone footage from a tender, if he has any. It will look better than stock and carries no clearance question.

## Frame 06 · 0:26–0:31 · b-roll
- **Pull:** Empty early-morning lane in a Greek or Adriatic town, shutters still down. Pexels or Unsplash: `Cyclades street early morning empty`.
- **Source / rights:** Pexels / Unsplash licence.

## Frame 07 · 0:31–0:37 · face
- **Shoot:** Same setup as frame 01, slightly tighter. The fair-minded line should be delivered to camera, not over b-roll.
- **Source / rights:** Jordan's own footage. Cleared.

## Frame 08 · 0:37–0:43 · face into slate
- **Shoot:** Hold on face, then cut to the rendered end frame.
- **Source / rights:** Jordan's own footage plus rendered slate. Cleared.

## Texture layer (optional, whole reel)
- Antique chart and engraving overlays at low opacity behind the text frames: Library of Congress maritime chart collection, or Rijksmuseum public-domain marine engravings.
- **Source / rights:** Public domain. Confirm the individual item's rights statement before use.

## Not to be used
- No cruise-line press-centre or trade-portal assets in this reel. Those libraries licence for editorial coverage *of the line*; this is advisor brand promotion, so it would need explicit written sign-off from the relevant BDM — and the reel does not need them.
- No passenger-shot social video of crowded ships or pier scenes, under any circumstances.
