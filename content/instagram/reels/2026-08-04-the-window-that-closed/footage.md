# Footage plan — the window that closes when you pay early

Reconstruct, don't repost. No client's booking, correspondence or confirmation
appears on screen in any form, redacted or otherwise — the pattern is described,
never illustrated with a real file. Nothing on screen carries a real figure.

**Rights basis, in order of preference:** (1) Jordan's own footage, (2) an
advisor-portal or BDM-supplied asset with written confirmation, (3) licence-free
stock. No passenger UGC, ever.

| Frames | Shot | Source & rights |
|---|---|---|
| 01–02 | Jordan to camera, desk, natural light. Same setup as the other reels in this series so the voice reads as one continuing column. | Jordan shoots himself. Cleared. |
| 03 | Insert: three cards laid down one at a time on a dark desk, each hand-lettered — "direct", "deposit", "paid in full". Props made for the shot, no line branding. | Jordan shoots himself. Cleared. |
| 04 | Hard cut to a closed door or a shut ledger — a single decisive movement for the turn. Shot in one take, no dialogue. | Jordan shoots himself. Cleared. |
| 05 | A desk calendar, month view, two dates circled in pencil. No year, no figures, nothing legible beyond the circles. | Jordan shoots himself. Cleared. |
| 06 | A telephone on a desk, still and silent, held slightly too long. The joke is the absence of a call. | Jordan shoots himself. Alternative: Pexels search "vintage rotary telephone desk" — Pexels licence, commercial use, no attribution required. |
| 07 | Antique chart or ledger texture drifting slowly under the text. | Library of Congress or Rijksmuseum public-domain maritime charts. Public domain; confirm the specific item's rights statement before use. |
| 08 | Insert: pencil ticking a date in the same calendar. Closes the loop with frame 05. | Jordan shoots himself. Cleared. |
| 09 | Back to face, framing matched to frame 01. | Jordan shoots himself. Cleared. |

**If a ship or harbour beauty shot is wanted under frame 05:** generic
coastline or berth footage only — Pexels search "harbour morning aegean" or
Coverr "sea from deck". Never stock footage of an identifiable named ship to
stand in for a sailing we sell. Line-supplied assets only via the advisor portal
or the BDM, and treat press-centre downloads as "confirm with BDM", not cleared.
