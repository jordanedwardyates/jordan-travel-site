# Footage plan — "The flight cancels, not the cruise"

Reconstruct, don't repost. There is no passenger footage in this reel and there must not be:
the viral missed-ship and airport-meltdown clips are owned by the individuals who filmed them,
no cruise line can license them, and Jordan's advisor permissions do not cover them.
Story is carried by Jordan to camera, static architecture, and on-screen text.

## Shot list

**01–02 · 0:00–0:07 · Jordan to camera (face)**
Source: Jordan shoots himself. Desk, evening lamp rather than daylight — the reel opens at night.
Rights: own footage.

**03 · 0:07–0:14 · Late-evening rebooking (b-roll, own)**
Source: Jordan shoots himself — a phone face-up on a dark desk, a hand not quite picking it up;
a notebook with a departure time written in ink. Do not show a real airline interface or a
recognisable app screen.
Rights: own footage.

**04 · 0:14–0:20 · Empty terminal / closed desk (b-roll)**
Source: first choice Jordan's own footage from any embarkation day — shuttered counter, empty
roped lane, nobody in frame.
Fallback: licence-free stock — Pexels search "empty terminal night no people", Coverr search
"empty hall night". Rights: royalty-free commercial use, no attribution required
(https://www.pexels.com/license/). No identifiable people, no airline or line branding.

**05–06 · 0:20–0:34 · The turn: the call being made (b-roll, own)**
Source: Jordan shoots himself — handset lifted, a pen ruling a fresh line, a boarding time
crossed out and rewritten. Frame ground flips to deep harbour on 04 and back to cream on 05,
so the turn reads with sound off.
Rights: own footage.

**07 · 0:34–0:41 · Quay at dawn (b-roll)**
Source: Jordan's own harbour material preferred — empty berth, bollard, mooring lines, first light.
Fallback: Pexels or Coverr, search "empty harbour berth dawn" / "mooring rope water".
Rights: royalty-free commercial use. Never use stock of a *specific named ship* to stand in for a
voyage we sell; keep hulls out of frame or unidentifiable.

**08–11 · 0:41–1:06 · Back to face (own)**
Same setup as frame 01 for the verdict and the rule. Hold the final frame a beat longer than feels
comfortable before the cut.

## If a line asset is wanted
Only via the trade/advisor portal or Jordan's BDM, and only as co-branded advisor-licensed material.
Press-centre libraries (Viking, Princess, Explora and the rest) license for editorial coverage of
the line, not for an advisor's own brand promotion — treat every press-centre file as
"confirm with BDM", never as cleared.

## Texture
Antique chart and engraving overlay: Library of Congress maritime charts and Rijksmuseum
public-domain prints. On-brand, free of stock gloss; verify the individual item's public-domain
status before use.
