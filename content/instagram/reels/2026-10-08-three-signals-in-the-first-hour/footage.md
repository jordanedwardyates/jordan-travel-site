# Footage — Three signals in the first hour

Face for 01, b-roll for 02–05, face for 06–07. ~38s, so cuts must be quick and clean.

## Frame 01 · 0:00 · face, 3s
- **Shoot:** Jordan to camera. Same seat and framing as the other two October reels.
  Rights: own footage.

## Frame 02 · 0:03 · the ready cabin, 6s
- **Shoot:** a made bed with a folded programme on it, or a turned-down corner and a
  reading lamp lit. Filmed by Jordan; a hotel room reads identically on camera.
  Rights: own footage.
- **Fallback stock:** Unsplash — *"made bed linen lamp"*. Licence-free, no attribution.
  Reject anything glossy or plastic-looking.

## Frame 03 · 0:09 · the carried bag, 6s
- **Shoot:** a soft holdall being lifted, hands only, no faces. Rights: own footage.
  No wheeled suitcases in frame — off-brand imagery.
- **Fallback stock:** Pexels — *"leather holdall hands"*. Licence-free.

## Frame 04 · 0:15 · the silent system, 7s
- **Shoot:** a static shot of an empty corridor or an unlit signage panel — silence, visually.
  Rights: own footage.
- **Texture alternative:** an antique engraved ship's-bell or speaking-tube plate from the
  Rijksmuseum public-domain collection. Public domain, and closer to the brand than stock.

## Frame 05 · 0:22 · the warn beat, 6s
- **Shoot:** a folded daily programme with the events page uppermost, defocused so nothing
  is legible. Rights: own footage. No readable prices, invented or otherwise.

## Frames 06–07 · 0:28–0:38 · face, 10s
- **Shoot:** back to the opening seat for the verdict, so the reel closes where it began.
  Rights: own footage.
- If any line-supplied embarkation footage is wanted instead, it must come from the
  trade/advisor portal or the BDM — **confirm with BDM before use**.

## Hard rules
- No passenger UGC.
- No named ship as a stand-in for a sailing we sell.
- No recognisable crew or guests without a release.
