# Footage — Four ways to miss your ship

**Reconstruction, not repost.** The viral "racing the gangway" clips circulating
online are filmed and owned by individual passengers. No cruise line can license
them and Jordan's advisor permissions do not cover them, so none of them appear
here. Everything below is either shot by Jordan or genuinely licence-free.

For the loop to work, the **first and last clip must be the same file**. Cut the
opening 3s and the closing 3s from one continuous take so the seam is invisible.

## Shot list

| Frame | Shot | Source & rights basis |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Jordan to camera, mid-shot, framed slightly off-centre against a cream wall. Says the hook with no preamble. Record one continuous 8s take; the top 3s is frame 01 and the tail 3s is frame 07. | **Shot by Jordan.** Own footage, no clearance needed. |
| 02 | Close, static, on a wristwatch being wound and set — hands only, morning light. Insert of a phone face down beside it. | **Shot by Jordan.** Film on any morning of a sailing; a domestic table works equally well. |
| 03 | Empty tender pontoon or floating platform with no ship in frame, water slapping the fenders. Static, tripod, 6s. | **Shot by Jordan** where possible. Fallback: Pexels video search "tender boat harbour swell" or Coverr "harbour water motion" — royalty-free, avoid identifiable branded hulls and recognisable faces. |
| 04 | Coach-window plate: dry inland road, olive or cypress country, no signage. Handheld from a seat is fine. | **Shot by Jordan.** Fallback: Pexels "empty rural road Greece" — licence-free. |
| 05 | A queue rendered abstractly: a stanchion rope and worn terminal floor, shot low. No faces in focus. | **Shot by Jordan.** Any ferry or terminal building will read correctly. |
| 06 | Overhead static of a printed daily programme on a bedspread, a hand photographing the port-agent line with a phone. | **Shot by Jordan.** Use a real programme from a past sailing, with cabin number and guest name masked. |
| 07 | Tail 3s of the frame-01 take. Identical framing, identical wardrobe — this is what makes it loop. | **Shot by Jordan.** Same take as 01. |

## Do not use
- Passenger-filmed pier-runner footage from any platform. Not licensable.
- Cruise-line press-centre video (Royal Caribbean, Princess, Viking asset
  libraries). Those grant editorial use *about the line*, not advisor brand
  promotion — **confirm with the BDM** before any of it is cut in, and treat it
  as uncleared until he replies in writing.
- Stock footage of a specific named ship standing in for a voyage we sell.

## Texture pass
Grade warm and slightly flat — printed, not glossy. If a background plate is
needed behind text, a public-domain maritime chart from the Library of Congress
suits this better than any stock harbour shot.
