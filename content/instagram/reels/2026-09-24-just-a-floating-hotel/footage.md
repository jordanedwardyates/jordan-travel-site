# Footage plan — "just a floating hotel"

**01 · 0:00 · face (3s)** — Jordan to camera, mid-close, slightly amused. Warn-tone frame over it.
*Source:* filmed by Jordan. *Rights:* own footage.

**02 · 0:03 · face (5s)** — Same setup, one step tighter.
*Source:* filmed by Jordan. *Rights:* own footage.

**03 · 0:08 · b-roll (7s)** — Interior warmth without branding: a single laid dining table, a lamp, a lounge chair
by a window with water beyond.
*Source:* Jordan's own from a ship visit is best. Fallback Pexels "hotel dining table window" —
choose a frame with no logos and no recognisable people.
*Rights:* own footage / Pexels licence, royalty-free commercial, no attribution.

**04 · 0:15 · b-roll (6s)** — Night sailing: dark water, town lights sliding past the frame. Then a morning quay

*Source:* Coverr "river night lights", Pexels "European quay morning".
*Rights:* Coverr / Pexels licence.

**05 · 0:21 · b-roll (5s)** — Three quick stills, one per city — a Vienna doorway, a Bratislava lane, the Budapest

*Source:* Unsplash stills, or Jordan's own photography.
*Rights:* Unsplash licence / own work. No identifiable faces in the foreground.

**06 · 0:26 · b-roll (7s)** — Deliberate contrast beat: an empty sundeck with two chairs, nothing happening.
The frame is the joke.
*Source:* Jordan's own; fallback Pexels "empty deck chairs river".
*Rights:* own footage / Pexels licence. Avoid anything reading as a beach or resort — brand rule.

**07 · 0:33 · face (7s)** — Back to Jordan for the verdict.
*Source:* filmed by Jordan. *Rights:* own footage.

**08 · 0:40 · face + card (7s)** — Hold, then closing slate over an antique river-chart texture.
*Source:* Library of Congress or Rijksmuseum public-domain collections.
*Rights:* public domain — verify the item's rights statement.

## Not to be used
- Passenger UGC.
- Cruise-line media-library footage of named river vessels; those assets are licensed for editorial
  coverage of the line, not advisor brand promotion — **confirm with the BDM in writing** first.
- Any resort, poolside or sun-lounger imagery, even ironically. It reads as exactly the thing the
  brand says it is not.
