# Three rivers, three completely different weeks

**Format:** listicle · **Runtime:** ~33s · **Words:** 87 (at 2.6 w/s)
**Hook (first 3s):** Three European rivers, three completely different weeks.

## Read-aloud script

> [0:00 — frame 01] Three European rivers, three completely different weeks.
>
> [0:03 — frame 02] The Rhine is the postcard one — castles stacked above the gorge, a lock every so often, and the shortest walk there is from ship to town square.
>
> [0:10 — frame 03] The Danube is the capital-city river. Vienna, Bratislava, Budapest, all on foot, often before lunch.
>
> [0:16 — frame 04] The Douro is the quiet one. Terraced vineyards, far fewer locks, small quays a large ship simply can't use.
>
> [0:23 — frame 05] Same word, cruise. Three different holidays.
>
> [0:27 — frame 06] Tell me which week you want and I'll tell you the river.

## Delivery notes
- Brisk. Three items, no preamble, no restating the premise between them.
- One beat of air between each river — the cut does the punctuation, not the voice.
- "Same word, cruise" is the dry line. Underplay it.
- Close warm, not salesy: an offer, not an ask.
