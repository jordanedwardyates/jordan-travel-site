## Not to be used
- Cruise-line asset-library video of named river vessels (Viking, AmaWaterways, Uniworld, Scenic).
  Those libraries licence for editorial coverage of the line — **confirm with the BDM** before using
  any of it in advisor-brand content.
- Passenger UGC of any kind.
- Stock footage of a specific named ship standing in for a sailing we sell.
