# Footage — three itinerary red flags

Fast cuts, one visual idea per flag. All owned or licence-free.

## Shot list

**01 · 0:00 — face, hook (Jordan, self-shot)**
Half-length at the desk, itinerary already in hand. Rights: owned.

**02-03 · 0:05-0:15 — flag one, the clock (Jordan, self-shot)**
Macro of a brass desk clock reading just before seven, then a pen circling a time on
a plainly generic, unbranded itinerary printout. No line name, no ship name, nothing
that reads as a real published sailing. Rights: owned.

**04-05 · 0:15-0:25 — flag two, tenders (stock)**
Pexels or Coverr search: "tender boat choppy water", "harbour swell wind". Avoid
identifiable livery and recognisable faces. Rights: Pexels/Coverr licence,
royalty-free commercial, no attribution required.

**06-07 · 0:25-0:34 — flag three, the sea day (Jordan, self-shot preferred)**
An empty deck chair and a book, mid-morning, nobody in frame. Fallback: Unsplash
search "open sea horizon from ship deck". Rights: owned, or Unsplash licence.

**08 · 0:34-0:38 — end card (texture)**
Antique Mediterranean chart plate behind the on-screen text. Source: Library of
Congress or Rijksmuseum public-domain maritime collections. Rights: public domain,
verify the individual item's record.

## Do not use
- Passenger UGC of any kind, including tender-queue and pier-runner clips.
- Cruise-line press-centre assets - editorial-coverage licence only; confirm with the
  BDM before use, never treat as cleared.
- Named-ship stock standing in for a sailing we sell.
