# Three itinerary red flags

**Format:** listicle · **Runtime:** ~38s · **Words:** 99 (at 2.6 w/s)
**Hook (first 3s):** Three things I check before I read a single port name on a cruise itinerary.

## Read-aloud script

> [0:00 — frame 01] Three things I check before I read a single port name on a cruise itinerary.
>
> [0:05 — frame 02] One. Arrivals before eight in the morning, or departures before four.
>
> [0:10 — frame 03] A seven-to-one call is half a day, and the boats eat the rest.
>
> [0:15 — frame 04] Two. More than two tender ports in a single week.
>
> [0:20 — frame 05] Tendering is the first thing weather takes away. The fare doesn't move.
>
> [0:25 — frame 06] Three. No sea day at all, or one parked on day two.
>
> [0:30 — frame 07] Placed before anyone needs it. It wastes the best day of the week.
>
> [0:34 — frame 08] Any one is survivable. All three and you've bought a schedule. Save this.

## Delivery notes
- Brisk. This is a checklist, not an essay - no dwelling.
- Number each flag out loud so the count is audible when muted.
- Drop half a beat before 'and the fare doesn't move'.
- Final line flat and unbothered. The save is the ask; don't sell it.
