# Footage plan — three lines to read on a cruise quote

No real quote appears on camera. Any document in frame is a mock printed for
the shoot: no line name, no logo, no legible figures, no client details. That is
both a privacy rule and a compliance one.

**Rights basis, in order of preference:** (1) Jordan's own footage, (2) an
advisor-portal or BDM-supplied asset with written confirmation, (3) licence-free
stock. No passenger UGC, ever.

| Frames | Shot | Source & rights |
|---|---|---|
| 01–02 | Jordan to camera, close, desk edge in frame. Sets the series look. | Jordan shoots himself. Cleared. |
| 03 | Overhead: a printed mock quote on a desk, a finger tracking down the page, pencil alongside. Body text set as unreadable filler at print size. | Jordan shoots himself. Cleared. |
| 04 | Insert: two identical printed cards side by side, one marked "refundable", one "non-refundable", in his own hand. | Jordan shoots himself. Cleared. |
| 05 | A desk calendar with one date circled in pencil. No year, no figures. | Jordan shoots himself. Cleared. |
| 06 | Back to face for the verdict, framing matched to frame 01. | Jordan shoots himself. Cleared. |

**Background texture, if wanted:** engraved chart or compass-rose scan at low
opacity behind the overhead shots — Library of Congress or Rijksmuseum
public-domain maritime collections, confirmed item by item. Preferable to stock
paper textures, which look glossy on export.

**Fallback b-roll only if the desk shots fail:** Pexels search "harbour dawn
stone quay" or Unsplash "Aegean coastline morning" — licence-free, commercial
use, no attribution required. Never an identifiable named ship, and never a
recognisable person without a release.
