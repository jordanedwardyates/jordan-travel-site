# Footage — The worst port-day plan I was sent this year

This is a critique of a decision, so the visual job is to make the arithmetic
legible, not to dramatise anybody's bad day. No passenger UGC anywhere in this
cut — the pier-runner and stranded-guest clips circulating online belong to the
passengers who filmed them and cannot be licensed by us or by any cruise line.

Neither the port nor the client is identified on screen. If a real itinerary
page appears in shot, mask the ship name, booking reference and guest name.

## Do not use
- Any passenger-filmed missed-ship or gangway footage. Not licensable, full stop.
- Cruise-line press-centre or asset-library video. Those libraries license
  editorial coverage *of the line*, not advisor brand promotion — **confirm with
  the BDM in writing** before using a frame of it. Treat as uncleared by default.
- Stock footage of a specific named ship standing in for a real sailing.

## Texture pass
Keep the grade warm and matte. Sound: room tone and water only — no music bed
under the numbers, so frame 04 lands in near silence.
