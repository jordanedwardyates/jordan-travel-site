# Three months I'd talk you out of

**Format:** listicle · **Runtime:** ~38s · **Words:** 100 (at 2.6 w/s)
**Hook (first 3s):** "Three months in the Mediterranean I'd talk you out of, and the sea is not the reason."

## Read-aloud script

> [0:00 — frame 01] Three months in the Mediterranean I'd talk you out of, and the sea is not the reason.
>
> [0:03 — frame 02] Late August in the Aegean. The meltemi is funnelling down every afternoon and every ship is still deployed.
>
> [0:09 — frame 03] Mid-October, if you came to swim. The light turns lovely and the tavernas begin closing.
>
> [0:15 — frame 04] And any month with a named weather season. Monsoon and hurricane windows are published. They are not surprises.
>
> [0:22 — frame 05] The fix is almost never a different ship.
>
> [0:26 — frame 06] It is one week. September holds the warmest sea of the year, and the wind has eased.
>
> [0:34 — frame 07] Sail with someone who reads the calendar.

## Delivery notes
- Flat and unhurried. This is a list, not a warning — the authority comes from how little you push.
- Land "and the sea is not the reason" dry, then a full beat of silence before the first item.
- Slight lift on "one week" — that is the whole argument and it should be the only emphasis in the read.
- Do not soften the third item into a disclaimer. Say it as a fact and move on.
- Face for 0:00–0:03 and again from 0:26 to the end; b-roll through the three items.
