# Footage plan — Three months I'd talk you out of

Rights basis is stated for every clip. Nothing here uses passenger UGC, and no clip
identifies a named ship.

## 01 · 0:00–0:03 · face
- Jordan to camera, chest-up, seated, cream wall or bookshelf behind. Shot by Jordan.
  **Rights: own footage.** First suggestion and the correct one — the hook needs a face.

## 02 · 0:03–0:09 · Aegean wind
- Wind-textured sea surface, whitecaps building, mid-afternoon light. Handheld from a
  headland or deck. **Rights: own footage** if Jordan has Cyclades material.
- Fallback: Pexels video search `aegean sea wind waves` or `greek island windy sea`.
  **Rights: Pexels licence — royalty-free, no attribution.** Avoid any frame with a
  branded hull or a recognisable face.

## 03 · 0:09–0:15 · turning season
- A shuttered taverna front, stacked chairs, low golden light. Static, tripod.
  **Rights: own footage** preferred; this is a shot worth making on the next October trip.
- Fallback: Unsplash stills `closed taverna greece` or `cyclades october light`, held as
  slow push-ins. **Rights: Unsplash licence.**

## 04 · 0:15–0:22 · named weather season
- Deliberately unglamorous: a chart or barometer detail, rain on glass, an empty berth.
- Preferred source for the chart texture: Library of Congress or NOAA public-domain
  nautical chart imagery, filmed as a slow pan across print. **Rights: public domain.**
  On-brand — antique chart rather than a weather-app screengrab.

## 05 · 0:22–0:26 · the pivot
- Cut back toward face, or a single static gangway/quay frame. Shot by Jordan.
  **Rights: own footage.** The turn in the argument should be visible when muted.

## 06 · 0:26–0:34 · September
- Warm flat sea, swimmers' eye level, late-afternoon light; a small harbour at ease.
  **Rights: own footage** preferred. Fallback: Coverr `calm sea sunset loop`.
  **Rights: Coverr licence — free for commercial use.**

## 07 · 0:34–0:38 · close
- Back to face for the verdict, then the letterpress end card from `render.mjs`.
  **Rights: own footage / own assets.**

## Do not use
- Any cruise-line press-centre asset here without checking first. Most press libraries
  licence for editorial coverage *of the line*, not advisor brand promotion — **confirm
  with the BDM** before any line asset appears in this reel.
- Passenger-filmed clips of windy tender operations. Owned by the passenger, not
  licensable by anyone else.
