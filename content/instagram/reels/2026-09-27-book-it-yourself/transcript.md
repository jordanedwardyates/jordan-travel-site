**Hook (first 3s):** You can book the cruise yourself. Nobody's pretending otherwise.

## Delivery notes
- Open flat and unbothered — this is a shrug, not a defence. No rise at the end of line one.
- Small pause before "it keeps it." That is the line people replay.
- "That's the part that annoys me" is the only warm beat; say it dry, half a smile.
- Land the last line quietly, as an offer rather than an ask.
