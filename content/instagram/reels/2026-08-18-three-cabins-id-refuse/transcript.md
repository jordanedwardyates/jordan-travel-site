>
> [0:03 — frame 02] One. Anything directly beneath the pool deck. Loungers on steel at six every sea day.

## Delivery notes

- Count "one", "two", "three" cleanly with a small pause after each number — that pause is what makes it saveable.
- "You don't get to choose who's behind it" is the dry line. Underplay it.
- Last line is an instruction, not a plea. Even tone, then stop.
