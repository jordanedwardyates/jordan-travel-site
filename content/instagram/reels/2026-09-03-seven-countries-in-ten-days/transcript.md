# We did seven countries in ten days

**Format:** reaction · **Runtime:** ~48s · **Words:** 124 (at 2.6 w/s)
**Hook (first 3s):** Someone told me last week they'd done seven countries in ten days. They said it like a win.

## Read-aloud script

> [0:00 — frame 01] Someone told me last week they'd done seven countries in ten days. They said it like a win.
>
> [0:06 — frame 02] I asked what they remembered. They named two places. The rest was harbours and coach windows.
>
> [0:12 — frame 03] That's not their fault. It's what the itinerary was built to produce.
>
> [0:17 — frame 04] Seven countries in ten days means six early starts, six transfers and six all-aboard times.
>
> [0:23 — frame 05] By the fifth port you go ashore because it's paid for.
>
> [0:28 — frame 06] And the ship you chose so carefully, you never used. No long dinner, no afternoon on deck.
>
> [0:34 — frame 07] Four countries, two overnights, one sea day mid-week. You'd remember all four.
>
> [0:39 — frame 08] Fewer ports isn't less holiday. It's the only version you come home rested from.
>
> [0:43 — frame 09] Send this to whoever you'd share a cabin with.

## Delivery notes
- Start conversational, almost mid-thought - this is a story someone told you.
- 'They named two places' is the pivot. Say it plainly and leave a gap after it.
- Never sound superior about the traveller; the itinerary is the target, not them.
- Last three beats warm and slower. The send ask is one line, then stop.
