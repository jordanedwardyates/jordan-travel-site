# Footage plan — the low-water coach transfer

Reconstruct, don't repost. No passenger UGC of stranded sailings — those clips are owned by
the passengers who filmed them and cannot be licensed by anyone else.

## Shot list

**01 · 0:00 · face (3s)** — Jordan to camera, half-frame left, desk and chart behind.
*Source:* filmed by Jordan. *Rights:* own footage.

**02 · 0:03 · b-roll (5s)** — Slow static of a European river surface, mid-channel, no vessel in shot.
*Source:* Pexels video search "river water surface slow" or Coverr "calm river".
*Rights:* Pexels / Coverr licence, royalty-free commercial, no attribution. Avoid identifiable ships and faces.

**03 · 0:08 · face (4s)** — Cut back to Jordan, tighter. Same framing as 01 so the cut reads as emphasis.
*Source:* filmed by Jordan. *Rights:* own footage.

**04 · 0:12 · insert (5s)** — Overhead of printed terms and conditions, a finger tracking down a clause.
Shoot a real generic booking document with the line's name out of frame.
*Source:* filmed by Jordan. *Rights:* own footage. Do not show any line's branding.

**05 · 0:17 · b-roll (8s)** — Two beats: a river lock chamber with the gates closing, then a static of an
empty coach parking bay or a quayside road. The pairing carries the whole point without narration.
*Source:* Pexels "canal lock gates" and "empty coach park"; or Jordan's own from any European river trip.
*Rights:* Pexels licence / own footage.

**06 · 0:25 · b-roll (5s)** — Sundeck rail, low bridge passing overhead. Generic vessel, no livery visible.
*Source:* Jordan's own preferred; fallback Pexels "river cruise sundeck" — screen the clip for branding.
*Rights:* own footage or Pexels licence.

**07 · 0:30 · face (8s)** — Jordan, straight to lens, no movement. This is the beat that gets screenshotted.
*Source:* filmed by Jordan. *Rights:* own footage.

**08 · 0:38 · face + card (9s)** — Hold on Jordan, then the closing slate over a still of a maritime chart.
*Source:* chart texture from the Library of Congress or Rijksmuseum public-domain collections.
*Rights:* public domain — confirm the specific item's rights statement before use.

## Not to be used
- Any cruise-line video of a named river vessel. Line press-centre and media-library assets
  (Viking, AmaWaterways, Uniworld, Scenic) licence for editorial coverage *of the line*, not for
  advisor brand promotion — **confirm with the BDM in writing** before any of it appears here.
- Anything implying a specific line handled low water badly. The critique is of disclosure practice
  across the category, not of a named operator.
