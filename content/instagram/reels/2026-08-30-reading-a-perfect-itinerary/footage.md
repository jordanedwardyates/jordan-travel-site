## Do not use
- Passenger-filmed pier-runner or tender clips. Owned by the individuals who shot
  them; no line can license them and advisor permissions do not cover them.
- Cruise-line press-centre or trade assets here. Most licence for editorial coverage
  *of the line*, not advisor brand promotion - confirm with the BDM before any use.
  Not cleared by default.
- Stock footage of a specific named ship standing in for a sailing we sell.
