# Footage plan — The right ship for the wrong person

**Reconstruct, don't repost.** This is a post-mortem about a real pattern, so the visual grammar is Jordan to camera, generic sea-and-harbour b-roll, and on-screen text carrying the story. No named ship, no line livery, no identifiable travellers at any point — the couple in this story must not be locatable from a single frame.

Face for 0:00–0:12 and again from 0:57. B-roll through the body. Pure talking head would not survive 69 seconds.

## Frames 01–02 · 0:00–0:12 · face
- **Shoot:** Jordan to camera, seated, chart or letterpress print behind, window light from the left. Slightly wider than usual — this is a story, not a list.
- **Source / rights:** Jordan's own footage. Cleared.

## Frame 03 · 0:12–0:19 · b-roll
- **Shoot:** A printed itinerary or port list on a desk, a finger tracking down the column of place names. Redact or obscure any line branding on the page.
- **Source / rights:** Jordan's own footage. Cleared.

## Frame 04 · 0:19–0:27 · b-roll, the turn
- **Pull:** A small boat launched or crossing cold open water in flat early light. Pexels search: `small boat cold sea grey morning`.
- **Source / rights:** Pexels licence — royalty-free, commercial use, no attribution. Reject any clip showing a hull name, funnel logo or branded expedition jacket.
- **Preferred:** Jordan's own footage from an expedition tender if he has any.

## Frames 05–06 · 0:27–0:43 · b-roll, the mismatch
- **Shoot:** Two beats. A dinner table with a second bottle open and glasses half-full, warm lamplight, no people in frame. Then the same table cleared, room empty, single overhead light.
- **Source / rights:** Jordan's own footage. Cleared. Venue permission in writing if the room is recognisable.
- **Alternative:** Unsplash stills under the Unsplash licence, search `dinner table candlelight wine` and `empty dining room night`, each held with a slow push-in.

## Frames 07–08 · 0:43–0:57 · b-roll, the fix
- **Pull:** A stone quay at dusk with lit tavernas behind it; then a mooring line made fast alongside. Pexels: `harbour town evening quayside lights`, `mooring rope bollard quay`.
- **Source / rights:** Pexels licence. Generic harbours only — do not use a shot that identifies a specific vessel.
- **Preferred:** Jordan's own Aegean or Adriatic harbour footage. This is the emotional payoff frame and his own material will carry it better than stock.

## Frames 09–10 · 0:57–1:09 · face into slate
- **Shoot:** Back to the frame-01 setup, tighter, for the verdict and the question. Then cut to the rendered end frame.
- **Source / rights:** Jordan's own footage plus rendered slate. Cleared.

## Texture layer (optional, whole reel)
- Antique chart and marine-engraving overlays at low opacity behind the warn frames: Library of Congress maritime chart collection, Rijksmuseum public-domain marine engravings.
- **Source / rights:** Public domain — check each item's individual rights statement before use.

## Not to be used
- **No passenger UGC of any kind.** Not expedition landing clips, not dining-room video, not anything sourced from social. Those clips are owned by the individuals who filmed them, no line can license them, and Jordan's advisor permissions do not extend to them.
- **No cruise-line press-centre or trade-portal assets in this reel.** Even where a line's expedition footage would fit perfectly, those libraries license for editorial coverage of the line — using them under a story about a ship being the wrong fit would need explicit written sign-off from the relevant BDM, and should not be sought for this piece.
- No stock footage of a specific named ship standing in for the vessel in the story. That is both a brand and a compliance problem.
