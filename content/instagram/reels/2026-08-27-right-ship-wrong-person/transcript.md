# The right ship for the wrong person

**Format:** post-mortem · **Runtime:** ~69s · **Words:** 179 (at 2.6 w/s)
**Hook (first 3s):** "The ship was excellent. It was the wrong ship, for the right reasons — and that's the mistake I see most."

## Read-aloud script

> [0:00 — frame 01] The ship was excellent. It was the wrong ship, for the right reasons — and that's the mistake I see most.
>
> [0:05 — frame 02] A couple came to me after a voyage they'd booked themselves. Expedition ship, magnificent coastline, genuinely serious naturalists aboard.
>
> [0:12 — frame 03] They'd chosen it off the route. The route was the best thing on the page.
>
> [0:19 — frame 04] But an expedition ship is built around the boats. Landings start early, so dinner is early, and the evening is over by nine.
>
> [0:27 — frame 05] And these two travel for the evenings. Long dinners, a second bottle, somewhere to sit and argue about the day.
>
> [0:35 — frame 06] Nothing failed. The crew were superb, the landings were the real thing. They simply weren't the people the ship was designed for.
>
> [0:43 — frame 07] The fix wasn't a different route. It was a yacht-style ship on almost the same coast — late dinner, marina at the stern, alongside in small harbours.
>
> [0:52 — frame 08] Same scenery. Completely different week.
>
> [0:57 — frame 09] So choose the style before the itinerary. Route is the easy part to fix.
>
> [1:03 — frame 10] Send me the two you're weighing up and I'll tell you which one is you.

## Delivery notes

- The first line is a concession, not an accusation. Warm. The point of this reel is that nobody did anything wrong — a well-designed ship met a traveller it wasn't designed for.
- Never characterise the couple as having made a foolish choice; the route genuinely was the best thing on the page. Keep the critique on the *matching*, not on them and not on the ship.
- Pause after "the evening is over by nine". That silence is the turn.
- "Nothing failed" should be said plainly and slightly quietly — it is the honest centre of the story.
- Lift on the fix. "Same scenery. Completely different week." lands as two separate sentences with a real gap between them.
- Final line: dry, no urgency, no sell.
