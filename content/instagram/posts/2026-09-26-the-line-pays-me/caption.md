How a luxury cruise advisor is actually paid — and why your fare is not the thing that changes.

Cruise lines build the cost of distributing a fare into the fare itself. It is spent whether an advisor holds your booking or nobody does. If nobody does, the line keeps it.

So the arrangement is simpler than people assume: the line pays me, after you sail. Your Mediterranean or Greek islands fare is the same or better than booking direct, never marked up, and the planning, the cabin selection, the fare-watching and the phone call in a crisis are complimentary.
