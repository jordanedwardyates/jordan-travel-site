Cabin choice is the one part of a cruise booking that only ever gets worse the longer you wait.

Ships, fares and itineraries hold for months. Position doesn't. The quiet midship cabins go first, and they go to the people who asked for them by name — above and below checked, connecting door refused, view unqualified.

So book the ship early for the cabin, not for the fare. A fare can be watched and repriced downwards after you've booked; I do it as a matter of course. A cabin position cannot be recovered once the deck plan has filled, whichever season you're sailing.

Send this to whoever you'd share a cabin with.
