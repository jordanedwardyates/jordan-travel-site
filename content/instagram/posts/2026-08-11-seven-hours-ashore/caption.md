A seven-hour call in port is really about four hours ashore. The rest is arithmetic.

Take an hour off the front for disembarkation and the terminal queue, and an hour off the back because all-aboard sits thirty to sixty minutes before the ship sails. What is left is the day. Plan one thing in it, not three.

The other half of this is timing. In a Greek islands or Adriatic summer, the crowd is a scheduling fact — two ships alongside at ten means the old town is yours at nine and nobody's at noon. Walk off late, eat inland, turn around early.
