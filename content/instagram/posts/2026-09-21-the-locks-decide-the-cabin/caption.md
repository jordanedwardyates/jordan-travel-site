Your river cabin is the size it is because of a lock built two centuries ago.

River vessels are dimension-limited: length, width and height are all set by the smallest lock and lowest bridge on the route, not by what a designer would prefer. That is why the ships are long and low, why there is one dining room rather than six, and why the suites are modest by ocean standards.

What the space is spent on instead is the window. French balconies, full-height glass, and a sundeck where the whole week actually happens between locks.
