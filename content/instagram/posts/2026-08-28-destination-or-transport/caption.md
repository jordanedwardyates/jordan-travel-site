One question sorts most of the shortlist: is the ship the destination, or is it the transport?

Sea days are either the point or the penalty, and no ship is neutral about it. A ship built to hold you for two days at sea has a library, deep lounges and long lunches. A ship built to move you between Adriatic harbours does not need any of that, and charges accordingly.

The itinerary confesses which one you are buying. Count the sea days. Look for an overnight in port. Note the departure times — anything after six turns dinner ashore into a real evening rather than a negotiation.
