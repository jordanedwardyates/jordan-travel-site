A river week has no sea day — which means it also has no day where nothing is expected of you.

Ocean itineraries build sea days in on purpose: the spa, a long lunch, a book, a passage you are meant to enjoy rather than endure. On a river the ship arrives somewhere every morning, the guided walk starts early because it sails again in the afternoon, and there is always something worth seeing.

Seven towns in seven days is wonderful and it is genuinely tiring. The fix is simple: skip one excursion deliberately. The sundeck between locks is the sea day you didn't get.

Save this if you are choosing between the two.
