Gold leaf is cheap. Space is the expensive part, and it is the one thing a brochure photograph cannot show you.

A grand staircase costs a line once. Carrying four hundred guests where a competitor carries nine hundred costs it every single sailing, forever. That is why the quiet ships are the expensive ones and the gilded ones often aren't.

So when I read a ship, I read the arithmetic first — guests against tonnage, guests against crew, seats in the dining room against beds in the cabins. Then I look at the pictures.

An October week in the Cyclades on a small ship is a different holiday from the same week on a large one. Not grander. Emptier, which is better.
