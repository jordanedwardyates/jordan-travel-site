A guarantee fare guarantees a category. It does not guarantee a cabin — and the difference is your whole week.

When you take the assigned rate, the line gives you what remains after everyone who chose has chosen. What remains is, by definition, the cabin nobody wanted: under the pool deck, beside a service door, sharing a connecting door with strangers.

There are honest exceptions. A three-night sailing in sheltered water, a near-full ship, a deep off-season fare where the gap is genuinely large. Anything longer than a week, no — you live with that position for too many nights to gamble on it.

Send this to anyone holding a guarantee fare.
