The best month to sail the Greek islands is usually the second-best month — and it costs less.

Peak season in the Aegean is peak because of the school calendar, not the weather. July and August bring the heat, the crowds at Santorini, and the meltemi at its strongest in the afternoons. September gives you the warmest sea of the year, an easing wind, and towns that have exhaled.

The weeks I actually watch are the shoulders: the last week of May, the first two of October. Softer fares, quieter marquee ports, and the islands briefly belonging to the people who live there.
