Included excursions are not free excursions. They are in the fare — the question is whether they're the ones you'd choose.

A river fare usually covers a guided walk a day, a genuinely expert local guide and the coach. That is real value, and on most days I would take it. But it is designed for a hundred people at once, which is exactly why it cannot serve a specialist interest — a quinta table on the Douro, a garden, a cathedral organ.

Take the included tour on the days you have no strong opinion. Spend on the two or three where you do.
