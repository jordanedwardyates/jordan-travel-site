Five questions I ask before I will name a single ship — the brief matters more than the brochure.

Two travellers can want the same Aegean coastline in the same shoulder-season week and need entirely different ships. Naming the ship first is how people end up on the wrong one.

What I actually need is temperament: what you last regretted, how many ports before it stops being a holiday, whether dinner is at eight or whenever, who else is coming, and what would make you feel oversold. The answers decide the line, the ship and the season. The cabin comes last.

Answer the first one in the comments — what did you last regret on a voyage?
