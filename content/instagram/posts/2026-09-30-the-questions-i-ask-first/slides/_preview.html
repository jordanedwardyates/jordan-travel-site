Where I start

The brief

Five questions before I name a ship

01
What did you last regret?
The clearest signal in the whole conversation. Regret is far more specific than preference.
02
How many ports is too many?
Some want seven. Some want three and a long lunch. No correct answer, only yours.
03
Dinner at eight, or whenever?
Fixed seating, open dining and a small ship’s single restaurant are three different holidays.
04
Who else is coming?
A cabin for two is a different problem to a family across three cabins on separate decks.
05
What would feel oversold?
Everyone has a line. Yours tells me which ships to leave off the list entirely.

The payoff

What the five answers decide

The line
Service style, temperament, and how much ceremony you can comfortably stand.
The ship
Size, motion, and whether it can berth where the large ships must anchor.
The season
Shoulder dates buy quieter ports more reliably than any upgrade ever will.
The cabin
Chosen last, on the deck plan, once the first three are properly settled.
