The bag you hand over at the kerb is not the bag you need for the first afternoon.

On embarkation day your suitcase is taken outside the terminal and delivered to the cabin later — sometimes well into the evening. So medication travels on your shoulder, in its original packaging with the pharmacy label attached, with a few spare days beyond the sailing. Anything about dosage or supply is a conversation with your doctor, not with me.

The same bag carries the passport, the documents and some local cash. In the Cyclades in September, on a tender day, the island taxi and the harbour café may take nothing else.

Everything else can wait in the hold until dinner.

Save this for the night before you pack.
