"Ocean view" is a promise about a window. It is not a promise about what is on the other side of it.

The category means glass in the hull. On the lower promenade decks of many ships, what fills that glass is a lifeboat, a set of davits or a steel rail — and the line discloses it in a single word most people scroll past.

Sometimes that cabin is the clever buy. On a Greek islands sailing where you are ashore by eight and back at six, an obstructed fare funds a better shore day. But if the sunrise over the Aegean is why you booked, refuse any category with a qualifier attached.
