The clock on your phone is not the clock that leaves without you.

Ships run on ship's time, and on a Mediterranean or repositioning itinerary that clock is deliberately nudged ahead of the shore. All-aboard is published in ship's time — normally thirty to sixty minutes before the ship sails. At a tender port, the last tender back is earlier still, and it is a separate number.

The captain is not being unkind. Berth slots and maritime requirements set the departure hour; waiting is frequently not something the bridge can offer.

So: set your watch at breakfast on the first morning. It is the whole trick.
