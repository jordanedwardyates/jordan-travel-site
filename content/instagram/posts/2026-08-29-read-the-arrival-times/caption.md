A seven-night Aegean itinerary is won or lost in the arrival and departure times, not the port names.

Seven ports reads better than four. Then you read the second column: two calls arriving at seven in the morning and leaving at one, two of them tender-dependent, one port a two-hour coach ride from the town it is named after.

All-aboard falls thirty to sixty minutes before departure and it is stated in ship's time, not the time on your phone. At an anchorage the last tender back is earlier still, and a separate time again.
