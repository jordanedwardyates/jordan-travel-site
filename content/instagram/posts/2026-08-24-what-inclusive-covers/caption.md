All-inclusive is a word, not a contract. Here is what it usually covers on a luxury cruise fare — and what it doesn't.

Broadly: every restaurant, a house wine and spirit selection at lunch and dinner, crew gratuities, and some Wi-Fi. Broadly not: flights unless stated, the reserve cellar list, spa and laundry, and private cars and guides ashore.

Before you compare two Mediterranean fares, separate the air. An included-air number and a cruise-only number are not the same measurement, and comparing them is how people talk themselves into the more expensive week.
