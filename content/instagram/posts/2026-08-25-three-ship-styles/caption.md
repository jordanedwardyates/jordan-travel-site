Expedition, classic ocean, yacht-style. Three quite different holidays, all sold under the same two words.

An expedition ship is built around the boats — landings start early, so dinner is early and the evening is over sooner than you expect. A classic ocean ship is built for sea days and structured evenings. A yacht-style ship is built for short quays, late dinners and a marina at the stern.

The style is the decision that a beautiful Aegean or Adriatic route cannot rescue. Nearly every disappointed traveller I inherit chose the route first and took whatever ship was sailing it.

Send this to whoever you'd share a cabin with, then tell me which of the three you think you are.
