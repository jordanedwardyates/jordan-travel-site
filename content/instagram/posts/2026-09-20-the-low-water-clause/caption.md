Nobody mentions low water at booking. It is the one thing that can rewrite a river week mid-sailing.

When a stretch of the Rhine or the Danube runs too shallow to transit, the line reroutes: guests coach to the next town, or two ships swap guests so neither has to cross the shallow reach. High water does it too — bridges and locks set a ceiling as well as a floor.

Water level is weather, and weather is nobody's fault. But every line words its low-water clause differently, and that difference only shows up in the week it bites. Ask at booking, not at the pier.
