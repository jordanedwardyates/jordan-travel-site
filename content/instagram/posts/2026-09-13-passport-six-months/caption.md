A passport can be perfectly valid and still be refused at check-in.

Many countries require six months of validity beyond your travel dates, and the rule varies by country and by nationality. On an Aegean or Adriatic itinerary you may be entering four or five jurisdictions in a fortnight — each has its own answer. Check them individually, then confirm with the line or your advisor rather than trusting a general rule.

The second failure is quieter: the name. If the booking says Elizabeth and the passport says Elisabeth, or a married name was added after deposit, that is a problem to solve in spring, not at the terminal.

Renewal timescales move. If yours expires within a year of sailing, start now.
