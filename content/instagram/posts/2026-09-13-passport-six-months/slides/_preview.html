Three documents worth checking today

01
The expiry date, in months
Count forward from your last day ashore, not from the sailing date.
02
The name, letter for letter
The booking must match the passport. A married name added later is the commonest mismatch.
03
Blank pages and condition
Some countries want blank pages, and a torn or water-damaged passport can be refused outright.
