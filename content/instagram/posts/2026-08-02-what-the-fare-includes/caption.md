Two Aegean sailings can look a week apart on price and finish level by disembarkation morning.

The fare is the entry price. Drinks, gratuities, transfers, the line's own shore excursions and the pre-cruise night in Athens all sit somewhere — either inside the fare or on the account you settle at the end.

An inclusive luxury fare isn't generosity. It's a line deciding not to sell you anything else once you're aboard, and that is a genuinely different holiday from one where every day carries a small transaction.

Cost both sailings to the same finish line and the choice becomes about the ship, which is where it belonged.
