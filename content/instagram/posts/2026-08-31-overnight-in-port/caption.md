Overnight in port is the most underrated line in a cruise itinerary, and almost nobody searches for it.

A ship that stays the night hands you the same Greek island twice: once in the afternoon when it belongs to the fleet, and once at nine in the evening when it belongs to the people who live there. Dinner ashore with no all-aboard over the second course. A morning in a square still being swept.

It is also weather insurance. Two windows instead of one, which matters enormously at a tender port.

Save this, then look for the word overnight on the next itinerary you are sent.
