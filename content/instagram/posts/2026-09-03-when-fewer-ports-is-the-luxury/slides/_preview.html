The urge to see everything is why most people see nothing.

Every additional port is another early start, another transfer and another all-aboard time. Add four of them to a fortnight and you have removed the holiday from it.

What fewer ports buys

Three things you get back

01
Time ashore that isn’t rationed
Two long days in one place beat four short ones. You get past the harbour and into the town behind it.
02
A ship you actually use
The library, the deck at five, a dinner that runs long. None of it happens on a six-port week.
03
Weather margin
Fewer, longer calls at alongside berths are much harder for a swell to take away from you.
