Fewer ports is the luxury. Seven countries in ten days is a schedule, and it is why people come home tired.

Each extra call on a Mediterranean itinerary costs an early start, a transfer, and an all-aboard time. By the fifth port most travellers go ashore because it is paid for, not because they want to - and the ship they chose so carefully goes unused.

Four ports, two overnights and a sea day mid-week is the version you remember all of. Small ships berth alongside where large ones must tender, which helps too.
