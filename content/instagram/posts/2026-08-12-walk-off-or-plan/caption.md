An itinerary prints a city's name. What it actually books is a berth, and the two are often an hour apart.

Civitavecchia is not Rome. Livorno is not Florence. Kuşadası is not Ephesus. Those are coach days, and they eat a Mediterranean cruise port call whole. Meanwhile Kotor, Valletta, Bordeaux and Chania hand you the town at the bottom of the gangway.

Four questions settle it: how far, alongside or tendered, how long in ship's time, and how many ships share the day. Sometimes the answer is to skip the famous inland thing and do it properly before the ship.
