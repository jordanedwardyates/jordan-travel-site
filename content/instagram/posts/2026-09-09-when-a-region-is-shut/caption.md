Some regions are simply shut in the month you were hoping to sail. I'd rather say so than sell you the week anyway.

Seasonality isn't only about crowds and fares. Ships redeploy. Polar seasons open and close with the ice. Monsoon and Atlantic hurricane seasons are published, predictable windows that lines plan around and travellers routinely book into. Greek island tavernas close for winter, and some smaller ports effectively stand down.

None of that is a reason to be afraid of a season. It's a reason to know which one you're in, what the itinerary does when weather intervenes, and what the ticket contract actually promises. Weather-driven port changes are the line's call, and refunds are usually limited to port fees.

Save this before you fall for a date.
