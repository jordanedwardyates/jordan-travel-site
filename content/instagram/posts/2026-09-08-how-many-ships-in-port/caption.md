Before you book a summer sailing, ask a question almost nobody asks: how many other ships are in port that day.

Your experience of Santorini or Dubrovnik is not set by your ship. It is set by the total number of passengers ashore in the same six hours — yours plus everyone else's. Port schedules are published well in advance, which means this is knowable before you pay, not a surprise you absorb on the day.

Two things change it. Sail in the shoulder weeks, when fewer ships are on the deployment. Or sail a small ship with an early berth and be walking out as the queues form.
