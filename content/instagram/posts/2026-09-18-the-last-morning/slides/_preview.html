Settle these first

Four things to arrange before you sail

01
The flight time, honestly
Ask your line what it advises for your port, then add margin for the customs hall on top.
02
The visa for a port you skip
Requirements can apply on arrival by sea even if you never intend to go ashore. Check each country.
03
The onboard account
Look at it two days out, not in the queue at guest services on the final morning.
04
A pre-arranged transfer
The taxi rank at a cruise terminal on a Saturday is a lottery you do not need.

The one people forget

Visas are itinerary-wide, not port by port.

Some countries require documentation from cruise passengers whether or not they disembark, and the rules depend on your nationality. Confirm each one individually.

The payoff

Fly home late, or stay a night. The city is worth the extra morning.

The logic that makes you fly in a day early makes you fly out a day late. Both ends of a voyage deserve a buffer.
