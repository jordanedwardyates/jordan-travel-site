Disembarkation morning ruins more voyages than embarkation day does.

The ship cannot release anyone until local authorities clear the vessel. Then there is a luggage hall sorted by tag colour, a customs queue, and a terminal emptying two thousand people into Athens or Barcelona at once. An early flight makes all of that adversarial. A late-morning flight, or a night in the city, makes it a slow coffee.

The other last-minute failure is documentation. Some countries require paperwork from cruise passengers whether or not you go ashore, and it depends on your nationality — so confirm each country on the itinerary rather than assuming a port you plan to skip is exempt.
