Specialty dining and the genuinely good excursions are decided before you board.

Booking windows open by fare, suite category or loyalty tier, and they differ by line — so ask when yours opens rather than discovering when it closed. On a Greek islands sailing in September, the small-group guide in Naxos and the boat in Milos go long before the day.

There is one hard reason to book through the ship rather than privately: on the line's own excursion, a late return is the line's problem. It waits, or it gets you to the next port at its cost. On an independent tour it is not required to wait, and the recovery is yours to fund. Knowing which port days have no margin is the job.

Save this before your booking window opens.
