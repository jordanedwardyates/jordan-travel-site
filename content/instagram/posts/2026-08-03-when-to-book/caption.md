Waiting for a last-minute deal works on a 4,000-berth ship. On a 300-guest one it mostly costs you the cabin.

A small-ship line has no distressed inventory to dump — it has a waitlist. The aft suites and the good midship categories go early, and shoulder-season Mediterranean dates go earliest of all.

So the honest answer to "when should I book a cruise" is: early for choice, then keep the booking under review. Fares move before final payment, and a watched booking can be rebooked when they fall. Nobody rings to tell you.
