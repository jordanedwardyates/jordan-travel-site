Two itineraries can list the same town and mean a five-minute walk or a forty-minute coach.

That is the first real difference between a river cruise and an ocean one. A river ship ties up alongside in the centre of Vienna or Budapest most mornings — you step off, and you can step back on for an hour and go out again. An ocean ship sometimes berths miles from the old town, and sometimes anchors offshore, which puts a tender queue and a separate last-tender time between you and the day.

Neither is wrong. But the docking plan is the itinerary, and it is the line nobody reads.
