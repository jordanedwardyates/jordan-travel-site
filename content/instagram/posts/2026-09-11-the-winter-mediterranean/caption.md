The winter Mediterranean isn't a worse voyage. It's a different one — and almost nobody is offered it.

Most of the fleet leaves for warmer water, so the sailings that remain are fewer and more deliberate. What's left is a Europe of cities rather than swimming: Rome, Barcelona, Valletta, Cadiz. Museums without queues. Restaurants that have their locals back. Short days, and rain that is a genuine possibility rather than a rumour.

It is the wrong choice if you came for the sea and the sun. It is the right one if you came for the shore, and if you would rather see a city than photograph a caldera.
