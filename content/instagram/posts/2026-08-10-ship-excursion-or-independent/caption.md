There is exactly one good reason to book the ship's excursion, and it isn't the guide.

If a cruise-line excursion runs late, the ship waits — or the line is responsible for getting you to the next port at its own cost. On a private tour, it is not required to wait, and every hotel, meal and flight needed to rejoin the ship is yours to fund. Same lateness, opposite outcome.

So the real question on any Mediterranean itinerary is margin. An hour inland, a tendered call, a short day, a border crossing: those are the days I book through the ship. A long call in a walkable port is where a private guide earns its keep.
