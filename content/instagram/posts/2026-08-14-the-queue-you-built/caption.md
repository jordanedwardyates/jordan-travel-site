The two-hour line at the cruise terminal is not malice. It is arithmetic, and everyone in it helped build it.

Arrival windows exist because security screening has a fixed throughput. Turning up early to be first aboard is what creates the queue you then stand in. Take the later half of your slot and walk on in ten minutes.

The stricter rule is the one for initial boarding — Royal Caribbean, for instance, requires guests aboard no less than an hour before departure. And the missed-embarkation stories that end badly are almost always flight delays, not late tours.

So: fly in the day before, and give the last morning a night ashore too.
