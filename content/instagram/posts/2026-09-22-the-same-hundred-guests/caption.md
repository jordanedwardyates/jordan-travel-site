A river ship's guest count is fixed for the whole week. That is either the best part or the worst.

One lounge, one dining room, one sundeck. By the second evening the crew know your name and so do the other guests, and you will walk the towns with the same group all week. For sociable travellers that is the reason to go. For anyone who wants to be anonymous at breakfast, it is the reason not to.

An ocean ship flexes to fit more kinds of traveller inside one sailing. A river ship does one thing, deliberately.
