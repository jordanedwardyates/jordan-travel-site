A cruise itinerary lists port calls. It does not tell you which of them are actually visits.

A call is a berth and a window of hours. Deduct the walk out of a working dock, the transfer to the town the port is named after, and the margin all-aboard demands, and a six-hour call in the Mediterranean can leave you two hours in the place itself.

Tender-dependent calls are looser still - swell or poor visibility suspends the boats and the port is missed with the ship anchored in sight of it.
