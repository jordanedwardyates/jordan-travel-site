Ship motion isn't luck and it isn't weather. It's where your cabin sits along the hull.

A ship pitches about a point near its middle. Forward cabins are the end of that lever and feel it first. Aft is calmer but sits over the propellers. Midship and low is the quietest ride on the vessel, and it is almost never the cabin anyone is excited to book.

So let the route decide. Summer Aegean, Adriatic, a Rhine or Danube river cruise: sit where you like, take the aft terrace. Open ocean, a repositioning leg, or a November crossing: midship, low, and put the difference into the shore days.
