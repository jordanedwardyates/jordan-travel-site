If your ship sails without you, exactly one phone number matters — and it is printed in the daily programme every morning.

The local port agent. Your passport is landed with them, and they work with the line and the local authorities to rejoin you at the next port. Photographing that page before you go ashore costs four seconds.

Two documented cases show what the agent cannot fix. Norwegian Dawn, São Tomé, 27 March 2024: eight guests on a private tour returned after the 3pm all-aboard and funded their own recovery. Disney at Dublin: 33 guests, equally late, but on the ship's own excursion — the line arranged their onward travel.

Save this. You will want it once.
