Who should not take a river cruise — and who, having done one properly, won't go back to an ocean ship.

I have talked people out of a river week and out of an ocean week in the same afternoon. Sea-day loyalists, families of six, and anyone buying the room rather than the route should stay on the ocean. Anyone who has walked off into Budapest after dinner, eaten at one good table every night, and never touched a suitcase between Vienna and Bratislava tends not to come back.

Get the format right and almost any well-run vessel will do. Get it wrong and the best ship on the water is still the wrong week.
