Free airfare, free drinks, free second guest. Nothing on a cruise brochure is free — it's priced in.

That isn't a scam, it's a preference. Lines would rather add than subtract, because a perk reads as generosity and a lower fare reads as weak demand. Both come out of the same margin, and only one of them is easy to compare across lines.

So translate before you compare. Strike out every inclusion you wouldn't have bought at its own price — the free airfare on a routing you'd never choose, the drinks package at your actual drinking pattern. What's left is the real offer.
