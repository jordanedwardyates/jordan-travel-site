The single supplement isn't a penalty for travelling alone. It's the price of a cabin that can't be re-cut.

A fare is quoted per person and sold per cabin. One occupant halves the revenue on a room the ship has a fixed number of — so the supplement exists, and pretending otherwise doesn't help anyone.

Where it softens is knowable: the handful of dedicated solo cabins on small ships, reduced-supplement promotions on sailings that need help, European river itineraries, and the quiet weeks — late autumn on the Danube, the Aegean before the crowds arrive.
