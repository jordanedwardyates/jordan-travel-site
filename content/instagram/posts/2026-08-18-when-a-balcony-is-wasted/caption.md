A balcony is not square footage. It's hours — and a port-a-day itinerary doesn't give you any.

Count the sea days before you pay the upgrade. On a seven-night Mediterranean cruise calling somewhere every morning, you are ashore all day and asleep by eleven. The veranda is decoration with a premium attached to it.

Where it earns its money: Norwegian fjords, scenic river cruising, an ocean crossing, warm nights at anchor in the Cyclades. Where it doesn't: northern Europe in October, six ports in seven days, or a lower-deck veranda with another deck cantilevered over the top of it.
