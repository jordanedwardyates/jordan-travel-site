Before you pay the upgrade

Four questions I ask first

01
How many sea days are there?
Fewer than two on a week-long sailing and the veranda is decoration you paid a premium for.
02
What is the month?
A Baltic balcony in October is a cold shelf behind glass. In June it is the best room you have.
03
Which side, and which way?
Scenic passages happen on one side. Knowing which is half the value of the position.
04
Is it overhung or overlooked?
A deck cantilevered above you takes the sky, the light and most of the point.

The payoff

Count your sea days. Then decide about the door.

Two or more, in a warm month, and the veranda is the best value on the ship. Otherwise take a quiet midship ocean view and spend the difference on a guide who knows the town.
