Draught, length, berth

What decides whether you dock

Quay length
A cruise berth is a finite piece of concrete. The longest ships get sent to anchor.
Water depth
Draught rules out some historic harbours entirely, regardless of how new the ship is.
Who booked first
Berths are allocated. Two large ships on the same day means one of them anchors.
Sea state
Tendering stops for swell and wind long before the sailing itself is at any risk.

The same Aegean call

Alongside vs at anchor

Alongside
  • Ashore by nine, easily
  • Back aboard for lunch
  • Weather rarely cancels it
  • No tender ticket, no group
At anchor
  • Tender groups and ticket times
  • Last tender: separate, earlier
  • Swell can cancel the day
  • Two hours lost to logistics
