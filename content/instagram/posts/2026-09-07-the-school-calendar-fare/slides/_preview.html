The trade

Term-time week vs free week

Term time
  • Peak fare, peak demand
  • Marquee ports at capacity
  • Restaurants booked out
  • Excursions sold through
  • Cabin choice already gone
Free week
  • Fare softens
  • Ports breathe
  • A table at short notice
  • Small groups ashore
  • Real cabin choice
