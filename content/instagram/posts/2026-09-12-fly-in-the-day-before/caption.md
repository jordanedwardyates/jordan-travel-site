Every missed-embarkation story I've handled began at an airport, not at a gangway.

A ship holds a departure slot set by the port authority and by maritime requirements. It is not being unkind when it sails — the bridge often has no discretion to wait. Your airline, meanwhile, owes you a rebooking, not a voyage.

The fix is unglamorous. One hotel night in Barcelona, Athens or Venice ahead of embarkation turns a cancelled evening flight into an inconvenience. Two nights if you are crossing an ocean. Bags can be rerouted overnight; they cannot chase a ship at sea.

Confirm what your fare's air protection actually covers with the line or your advisor — it varies more than people expect.
