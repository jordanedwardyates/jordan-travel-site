Refurbished last year can mean new corridor carpet. It can also mean every bathroom aboard. Both are true statements.

Hull age is close to useless as a signal. What matters is what the last refit touched: bathrooms and balconies first, then soft goods and lighting, then whether the public rooms gained venues without gaining seats.

There are places where new genuinely pays — suite bathrooms have improved more than anything else in twenty years of ship design, newer balconies are deeper, and newer propulsion is quieter aft, which is where the good suites tend to be. Expedition hardware has moved fastest of all.
