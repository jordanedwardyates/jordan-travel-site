Onboard credit is not money. It's a voucher for the line's own shop, and some of it expires unspent.

That's not a scandal — it's why lines offer it. Credit returns to them at retail, so they would rather hand you spend than cut the fare. It can still be genuine value, particularly on a mainstream sailing where the extras are where the cost lives.

Three pots exist and they stack: line promotional credit, agency credit, and loyalty or shareholder credit. Most travellers claim one. Then ask the two questions that matter — is it refundable, and is it per cabin or per person.
