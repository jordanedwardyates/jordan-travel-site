What guest count decides

One number, four consequences

Tender queues
More guests, more tender groups, and a longer wait for the same small boat.
Dinner at eight
Restaurant seats scale slower than cabins. Capacity is the constraint, not the menu.
Where you berth
Smaller ships fit alongside quays the largest ships must anchor off.
Crew attention
Fewer guests per crew member is the least glamorous luxury and the most reliable.

Read the specs

Three figures worth asking for

01
Guests at double occupancy
Not maximum capacity. Ask for both, and note the gap — that gap is your school-holiday sailing.
02
Guests per crew member
Published by most luxury lines. Lower is better, and it shows in the second week, not the first.
03
Space ratio
Tonnage divided by guests. A rough index of elbow room per person; compare it within a class only.
