Two ships can measure the same on paper and hand you completely different holidays. Guest count is why.

Three figures tell you more about a small ship cruise than any photograph: guests at double occupancy, guests per crew member, and tonnage divided by guests. None of them appear in the brochure headline, and all three are available if you ask.

They decide the things you actually feel — whether the ship ties up alongside in the Cyclades or sends you ashore by tender, whether dinner at eight is a booking window or a decision, whether the pool deck in August is a place you want to be.
