The trade

A non-refundable deposit is a discount you pay for in advance.

You are selling the line certainty about your cabin. That is worth something, and they price it. The question is whether you had certainty to sell.

Same cabin, two terms

What you are actually buying

Refundable
  • Higher fare
  • Deposit returns
  • Dates can move
  • Cancel and reconsider
  • Costs you money
Non-refundable
  • Lower fare
  • Deposit is spent
  • Change fees apply
  • Committed on day one
  • Costs you options

The dates that matter

Four dates to write down at booking

01
Deposit due
Usually days after booking, not weeks. Miss it and the cabin releases without ceremony.
02
Last day to reprice
In practice, before final payment. This is the window in which a fare drop is still useful to you.
03
Final payment
The cliff edge. After it the money is committed and cancellation penalties begin climbing.
04
Insurance cut-off
Some cover, particularly pre-existing conditions, must be bought within days of the deposit.

The payoff

Choose the deposit that matches your life, not the headline.

Fixed dates and settled health: take the lower fare. A parent unwell, a business mid-sale, a passport near expiry: pay for the exit and sleep.
