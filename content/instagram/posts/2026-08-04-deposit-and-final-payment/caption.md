Two identical cabins, two fares. The cheaper one is usually the one you can't walk away from.

A non-refundable deposit isn't a trick — it's a trade. You sell the line certainty about your cabin, and they price that certainty. The only real question is whether you had certainty to sell in the first place.

Four dates are worth writing down the day you book: deposit due, the window in which a fare drop can still be repriced, final payment, and the insurance cut-off — which for some cover is days after the deposit, not months.

Save this for the day you put a deposit down on a Mediterranean sailing.
