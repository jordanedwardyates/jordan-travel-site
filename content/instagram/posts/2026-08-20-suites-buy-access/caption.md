Suite categories sell square footage because it's easy to price. Access is what actually changes the week.

Some tiers give you a larger sitting room you'll use twice. Others change how the ship treats you: priority embarkation, priority tender at a Greek islands anchorage, a restaurant held for your deck, someone whose job is fixing things before you notice them.

The move, almost always, is the lowest rung of the access block rather than the top of the space block. And on a genuinely inclusive luxury cruise fare, check first — much of the suite benefit may already be yours, in which case you are only buying the room.
