Travel insurance is the one purchase in a voyage where the fine print is the product.

Two clauses do most of the damage. The first is the pre-existing-condition window: many insurers tie that cover to a period after your deposit date, and the length varies by insurer and by country. Read your own certificate — I won't quote a number I can't verify for your policy.

The second is evacuation. Getting someone off a ship in the middle of an ocean is the most expensive thing that can happen on a voyage, and the limit is often lower than people assume, or combined with the medical limit rather than separate from it.

Buy at deposit, not at packing. And put your questions to the insurer in writing.
