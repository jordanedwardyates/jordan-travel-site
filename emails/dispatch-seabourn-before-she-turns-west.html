Before the Quest Turns West

There is a particular hush to the Adriatic in October — the ferries thinned to nothing, the harbor towns handed back to the people who live in them, the light gone long and gold by four in the afternoon. Seabourn Quest carries barely 450 guests, and a crew that knows your name by the second night. This is the last month she holds it in these waters.

Three sailings from her are still open, out of fifteen left in the Adriatic and Aegean this year — I checked the board again this morning. The entry-level suite has already closed on thirteen of those fifteen; on one of my favorite Adriatic departures, the Veranda rate dropped $650 in the last seven days alone. I watch this every Monday so you don’t have to. Below is what’s still available, in the order I’d book it.
