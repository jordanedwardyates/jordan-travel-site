Jordan’s Take

If you’re staying in the Ocean Terraces, OT2 is the one I’d take — of the four suites I’m holding there, it’s the best-positioned run on the ship for this itinerary, and the gap between my price and theirs is the widest of the three Ocean Terrace grades. If you’d rather go up a tier, the jump from Penthouse to Deluxe Penthouse is where the value tightens: more room, a better set of suites to choose from, for a few hundred over PH. And if you want to go all the way, look hard at Grand Penthouse 10004 — it’s the only room on this list with an actual separate bedroom, and it carries the widest gap between my price and theirs of anything here, nearly $950 a person. If you were ever going to stretch a category, this is the sailing and the room to do it on.
