The Crossings & the Mediterranean

There is a particular quiet that settles over a ship mid-ocean — no port on the horizon, no schedule to keep, only days of open water between one continent and the next. The old crossings knew it. So do the few who still book them.

Seven voyages this week, all Oceania, all held. Three cross the Atlantic next spring — the old way to reach Europe, by sea. Four trace the Mediterranean this autumn, while the harbor towns still have their evenings. Ports, cabin sizes, and what I’d choose are below. Here is what I’d tell a friend.
