IV · Before You Sail

Passports. Valid six months past the end of the cruise. You come off in Rio on 26 February 2028, so you want them good through the end of that August. If either one runs out before then, renew now rather than in 2027 when everyone else is doing it.

Brazil visa. Brazil brought the requirement back for US citizens in April 2025, and since you disembark in Rio you will be entering the country properly. It is an e-visa, all online, through brazil.vfsevisa.com. VFS Global is the only outfit authorized to handle it, so ignore the other sites that turn up in a search offering to file on your behalf; they charge more for the same form. A Note From Jordan

Book the overnights, not the ports. Two full days in Punta Arenas and two in Buenos Aires are what separate this from a thirty night parade of tender stops. Punta Arenas is where you stage the Torres del Paine day, and being alongside in Buenos Aires until quarter to seven on the second evening means you can eat dinner at ten at night the way the city actually eats, then walk back to the ship. Most South America itineraries give you Buenos Aires for eight hours and call it done. Yours does not.
