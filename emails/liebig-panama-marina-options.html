The refit · confirmed

Marina heads into dry dock this October for the full OceaniaNEXT treatment, and it’s real work.

The cabins. Every stateroom gets redesigned — new layouts, fresh furnishings, and the bathrooms completely redone with marble and rainforest showers. A solid upgrade from what’s there now. The suites, Penthouse through Owner’s, all get the same care, so the whole ship feels intentional rather than patchy.

The public spaces. The Grand Lounge is being expanded, and they’re adding a new Founders Bar — basically a proper cocktail lounge built around craft spirits and small-batch work. All the dining venues, lounges, and main corridors get fresh carpet, lighting, and furniture. It reads considered.

Timing. She comes back into service late 2026 and sails straight into 2027 — so your March crossing steps aboard a ship that’s just had real investment. That’s worth leading with.
