The inaugural · why it isn’t just another sailing

The ship. Sonata is brand new, not a refit of something older. She carries 1,210 guests against 779 crew, which is close to three crew for every four passengers, and every stateroom aboard has its own veranda. You would be among the first guests ever to sail her.

The table. Two restaurants debut with the ship: La Table par Maîtres Cuisiniers de France, an intimate chef’s table built on French cooking, and Nikkei Kitchen, which is Peruvian-Japanese. The ones you already know come with her too: Polo Grill, Toscana, the Grand Dining Room, the Terrace Café, along with Horizons and the Founders Bar.

The occasion. A first season has an atmosphere that later ones don’t. The crew are new to her, the ship is dressed for it, and everyone aboard knows what they are part of. It only happens once, and I’d not be doing my job if I let it pass without a word.

Jordan’s Take

I’d take the A4, and I want to show my working. A4 and A3 are the same concierge level with the same privileges, but A4 comes in a hundred dollars a head under A3, so the more expensive of the two buys you nothing extra. And A4 sits only $150 a head above the standard veranda, which is a small sum for the concierge lounge and the better placement on a fourteen-night sailing. If it were Connie and me, I’d book 8005 and 8007 and not think about it again. Take A3 only if you see 8062 on the deck plan and simply prefer where it sits.

II · A Second Thought

EXPLORA I · Athens to Venice, ten days in October 2027
EXPLORA I · 10 days Departs Oct 18, 2027

Athens → Venice

Explora Journeys is the new luxury line from the MSC group, and it is a different animal from Oceania rather than a competitor to it. EXPLORA I carries 922 guests in 461 suites. Every one is a suite, every one has a balcony, and they average 453 square feet, which is a great deal of room by the standards of this end of the market. The crew ratio runs about one to every one and a quarter guests.

Nine places to eat are included in the fare, among them Fil Rouge, Anthology, Marble & Co Grill, Sakura and the Med Yacht Club. The spa runs to nine treatment rooms and a thermal suite, and there are four pools aboard, one of them under a roof that retracts when the weather cooperates. The whole thing is pitched quieter and slower than a conventional ship, which after fourteen busy days in the Mediterranean might be exactly the point.
