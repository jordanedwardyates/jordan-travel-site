Travel protection, by November 5

This one is just an idea, and it is yours to decide. Viking’s travel protection plan covers pre-existing medical conditions only if it is bought within 15 days of your first deposit. Your deposit went down October 21, so that window closes November 5. After that the plan can still be bought, up to final payment, but without that waiver.

Today only your $50 deposit is at stake, so it can feel early. The point of buying now is to lock in the waiver while you are both well; you need to be fit to travel on the day you buy it. The plan is $1,116 for the two of you.

If you end up not going, the cancel-for-any-reason part of the plan returns what you have paid as a Viking travel credit rather than cash. That lets you move to other dates or another Viking voyage. The credit is good for 12 months from when it is issued.
